What Bay Area Corporate Clients Order Most

Three product categories drive the majority of corporate volume:

  • Hand-piped royal icing logo cookies: Company logos are recreated by hand in royal icing — color matched, proportion accurate, and finished with enough detail that recipients recognize the brand immediately. Common uses include product launches, employee onboarding welcome kits, client appreciation gifts, and team celebration events.
  • Printed image cookies: Edible ink printing reproduces high-detail logos, photos, and branded imagery directly onto shortbread. High recognition across large quantities, with consistency from the first cookie to the last.
  • Corporate event cakes: Sheet cakes and sculpted cakes built around company logos, milestone messaging, anniversary numbers, retirement themes, and department-specific branding. Some orders are cleanly decorated cakes with a logo and a date; others are fully sculpted pieces tied to the company's product line or culture.

Departments within the same company frequently order independently — different floors, different event dates, different design briefs. The process accommodates this without requiring a single company-wide point of contact or approval chain.

The Corporate Ordering Process

The process is structured for people who manage vendor relationships professionally:

  1. Submit a request through the corporate order form at mybakingcreations.com/corporate-order.html — include the event date, estimated headcount, delivery address, and any branding assets you have available (logo files, hex codes, brand guidelines).
  2. Yana reviews the brief and responds with a custom quote scoped to the project — not a generic per-item price pulled from a public menu.
  3. The bakery handles all design interpretation and production. A proof or mockup is shared for approval before anything goes into production.
  4. Standard turnaround is one to two weeks for most items. Larger runs, sculpted cakes, or multi-product packages benefit from earlier requests. Rush availability can be discussed for established accounts.

Many corporate clients return for multiple events per year. Repeat accounts are welcomed, and order history on file makes subsequent projects faster to scope and approve.

Corporate Gifting That Goes Beyond the Office Party

Not every corporate order is tied to an all-hands event. Common use cases include:
