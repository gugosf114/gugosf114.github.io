How the Custom Cake Order Process Works

My Baking Creations is a made-to-order bakery, not a walk-in shop. Here is how the process works from inquiry to delivery:

1. Submit your order request. Use the order form at mybakingcreations.com/order-form. Include your event date, approximate guest count, and any design inspiration you have — a photo, a theme, a description.

2. Consultation with Yana. After reviewing your request, Yana follows up to discuss design details, confirm feasibility, and provide a pricing estimate. For most orders, this turnaround is 24–48 hours. There is no commitment required at this stage.

3. Design approval and scheduling. Once the design and pricing are confirmed, a deposit secures your date on the production calendar. Payment is accepted by credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Zelle.

4. Handcrafted production. Your cake is made from scratch — no mass production, no shortcuts. Sculpted and character cakes typically require 2–4 weeks of lead time. Standard custom cakes require a minimum of 1–2 weeks advance notice.

5. Delivery or pickup. San Ramon customers can arrange delivery directly to their home or venue. Pickup is also available at the main kitchen at 1096 Wildwood Ave, Daly City, CA 94015, or at the Outer Sunset SF location at 1800 23rd Ave, San Francisco, CA 94122.

Orders start at $150. This is a premium, made-to-order service. Pricing reflects the skill, time, and materials that go into every design.
