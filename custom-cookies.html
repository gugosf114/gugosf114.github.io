Hand-Piped Royal Icing Cookies: The Tier 1 Corporate Standard

Hand-piped royal icing cookies are the highest craft level in custom cookie work. Each cookie is decorated entirely by hand — no printing, no transfers — using royal icing applied layer by layer to reproduce logos, brand marks, and custom artwork with dimensional detail and a glossy finish.

The process:

  • Logo or design is reviewed and translated into a piping plan
  • Custom icing colors are mixed to match your brand palette (Pantone and hex references accepted)
  • Each cookie is flooded, detailed, and dried in stages — a process that can span multiple sessions per batch
  • Finished cookies are individually wrapped and packaged for distribution

Pricing: $6–$8 per cookie | Minimum order: 1 dozen (12 cookies)

Ideal use cases for corporate clients:

  • Product launches and feature announcements
  • Company milestone celebrations and work anniversaries
  • Employee onboarding welcome kits
  • Executive client gifting and deal-closing thank-yous
  • Holiday gifting campaigns
  • Team events and all-hands meetings

One important note for corporate buyers: individual departments at the same company order independently. There is no company-wide commitment required. Different floors, different teams, and different events at the same organization all place their own orders — this is standard practice with clients like Google, Meta, and Salesforce.

Printed Image Cookies: Logo-Perfect for High-Volume Corporate Gifting

Printed image cookies use food-safe edible ink printed onto edible paper, which is then applied to a smooth royal icing base on a shortbread cookie. The result is a crisp, photographic reproduction of your logo, product image, or brand artwork — accurate down to gradients, fine text, and multi-color details that would be difficult to replicate by hand.

Pricing: $5–$6 per cookie | Minimum order: 1 dozen (12 cookies)

When printed cookies are the right choice:

  • Your logo has photographic detail, gradients, or fine typography
  • You need a large quantity at a consistent quality level
  • You're building swag bags, conference giveaways, or trade show booth treats
  • You want fast production on a bulk run without sacrificing brand accuracy

How printed differs from hand-piped:

| | Hand-Piped | Printed Image |

|---|---|---|

| Best for | Dimensional artistry, bold logos, premium gifting | Photo-accurate logos, high volume, swag |

| Finish | Textured, organic, glossy icing | Smooth, flat, photographic |

| Price | $6–$8/cookie | $5–$6/cookie |

| Brand color matching | Custom icing mix | Edible ink print |

To place a printed cookie order, submit your logo file (PNG, SVG, AI, or EPS preferred) along with your brand color codes via the order form at mybakingcreations.com/order-form. A digital proof is provided before production begins.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between hand-piped and printed image cookies?

Hand-piped cookies are decorated entirely by hand using royal icing — each one is individually crafted with layered detail and a dimensional finish. Printed image cookies use food-safe edible ink to reproduce your logo or photo with photographic accuracy on a smooth icing base. Hand-piped is the premium choice for executive gifting; printed is ideal for high-volume corporate orders where logo accuracy and consistency matter most.

What is the minimum order for custom cookies?

The minimum order is 1 dozen (12 cookies), with a minimum order value of $150. My Baking Creations does not offer single-cookie or small-quantity orders below this threshold.

How far in advance should I order?

For standard orders, 1–2 weeks is the recommended lead time. Large corporate orders of 100 or more pieces should be submitted 2–3 weeks in advance. During peak periods — including May (Star Wars Day and graduation season) and November through December — earlier is always better.

Can individual departments at a company place separate orders?

What file formats do you accept for logo cookies?
