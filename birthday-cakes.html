How to Order a Custom Birthday Cake

The ordering process is straightforward:

1. Submit your order request — use the order request form to share your event date, guest count, and any theme ideas or inspiration photos you have

2. Receive a custom quote — Yana typically provides design sketches and pricing within 24–48 hours; no commitment is required at this stage

3. Approve the design — review the sketches, request changes, and confirm when the design is exactly right

4. Production begins — your cake is made from scratch, by hand, using fresh ingredients

5. Pickup or delivery — pick up at the Daly City kitchen (1096 Wildwood Ave, 94015) or arrange delivery across San Francisco and the Peninsula

A few things worth knowing before you order:

  • Lead time matters. My Baking Creations typically books 2–3 weeks in advance. For sculpted or complex designs, 3–4 weeks is recommended. The earlier you reach out, the more design options are available.
  • Minimum order is $150. This is a premium, made-to-order bakery — not a budget option.
  • Pickup is free from the Daly City location. Delivery fees vary by distance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order a birthday cake?

My Baking Creations recommends reaching out 2–3 weeks before your event for most custom birthday cakes. Sculpted 3D designs require 3–4 weeks. The earlier you contact us, the more flexibility there is on design and scheduling.

What information do I need to provide when ordering?

Your event date, approximate guest count, any theme or design ideas, and whether you prefer pickup or delivery. Inspiration photos are always helpful — the more detail you share, the better the initial sketch will be.

How much does a custom birthday cake cost?

Custom birthday cakes start at $250 and range to $650 and above depending on design complexity, size, and number of servings. Sculpted 3D cakes start at $300, with complex multi-element designs ranging from $400 to $650+. A detailed quote is provided after your design consultation.

Can I pick up my cake from the Daly City bakery?

Yes. Pickup is available at 1096 Wildwood Ave, Daly City, CA 94015, Monday through Saturday, 9am–6pm. Free street parking is available out front.

Do you deliver birthday cakes?

Yes. My Baking Creations delivers throughout San Francisco, the Peninsula, Silicon Valley, and Marin County. Delivery within 20 miles of the Daly City kitchen is included for sculpted cakes. Delivery fees for other orders vary by distance — contact the bakery for a quote.

Is there a minimum order?

Yes. The minimum order is $150. My Baking Creations is a premium, made-to-order bakery.
