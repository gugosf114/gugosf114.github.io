Corporate Cookie FAQ

How far in advance should I order corporate logo cookies?

My Baking Creations recommends ordering at least one week before your event. For large orders over 500 cookies, two weeks gives our team time to match your exact brand colors and print high-resolution logos on each cookie. Rush orders with same-day delivery are available for Bay Area offices.

What is the minimum order for corporate cookies in San Francisco?

My Baking Creations accepts corporate cookie orders starting at one dozen (12 cookies). Most San Francisco tech companies order 50 to 500 custom logo cookies for employee appreciation events, product launches, trade show giveaways, and client gifts. We handle orders of any size — from a boardroom meeting to a company-wide quarterly celebration.

Do you deliver corporate cookies to offices in San Francisco and Silicon Valley?

My Baking Creations hand-delivers custom corporate cookies, branded cake pops, and event cakes directly to offices and venues throughout San Francisco, Daly City, South San Francisco, Palo Alto, Mountain View, Cupertino, Sunnyvale, and San Jose. Every order arrives individually wrapped in event-ready packaging, presentation-ready, with boardroom setup available on request. Event-ready packaging means your EA can distribute without a project plan.

Can you print our company logo on cookies?

My Baking Creations offers two styles of corporate logo cookies: hand-piped royal icing designs for premium artisan gifts, and high-resolution edible image printing for pixel-perfect logo reproduction at scale. Companies like Google, Meta, and Salesforce use our printed logo cookies for employee onboarding, conference swag, and client thank-you packages — scalable from 24 to 2,400 pieces.

Do you accept purchase orders and invoices for corporate orders?

My Baking Creations accepts purchase orders, invoices, credit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Zelle. Our corporate ordering process is designed for procurement teams — we provide W-9s, itemized invoices, and Net 30 terms for established accounts.
