# Packaging film sample artwork

`sample-portraits.png` contains two fictional AI-generated sample portraits; these are not customer photographs. Created with the built-in image generator. Left panel: a warm close family portrait of two parents and their toddler in a garden. Right panel: the same baby's head-and-shoulders portrait on blush pink with space below for birthday text. No lettering was generated; the renderer adds editable text.

`google-g.svg` is the Google G asset from https://www.gstatic.com/firebasejs/ui/2.0.0/images/auth/google.svg, used as the requested corporate-cookie example.

The film artwork and three-row layout are defined separately from the packaging geometry, ready for real box-photo references.

Packaging geometry follows the white hinged box in local reference photos 1000001733.jpg through 1000001736.jpg (Downloads/box images). Original reference photos are not published. Window outline, depth, folding tabs, white crinkle filler and corner labels are modeled in order-packaging-box.mjs. The generated QR label links to the MBC homepage. The neutral oak serving table and props are illustrative. Film duration is 27 seconds, ending with the lid opening on the table.

Serving additions: the nine cake pops on the gold stand follow the disc-and-stick presentation in Downloads/2703.webp, with all three film designs repeated by column. One bitten Make-a-wish cookie sits on the stacked dessert plates. Every crinkle-paper vertex is bounded inside the white box walls.

Cake-pop correction: the three photos in Downloads/cake pops for astra (Alaska Airlines, DocuSign, Flying Princess) establish rounded dipped bodies, glossy red/yellow/pink coatings, smaller circular print wafers and long white sticks. The film retains its wish/baby/Google artwork, with yellow/pink/red coatings respectively.

Final cake-pop construction correction: heads are complete spheres. Thin circular fondant patches conform to the sphere and carry circularly cropped wish, baby and Google prints. There is no flattened cake-ball face or square print inset.

Resealable sleeve sequence: a three-sided clear pouch remains open at its mouth for insertion. Its extended back flap carries an adhesive band covered by a curling release liner; the liner peels away before the flap folds and seals. Every subsequent boxed cookie has a closed flap. The packaging and serving scenes move four seconds later; total duration is 31 seconds.
