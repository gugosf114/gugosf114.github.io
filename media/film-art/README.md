# Packaging film sample artwork

`sample-portraits.png` contains two fictional AI-generated sample portraits; these are not customer photographs. Created with the built-in image generator. Left panel: a warm close family portrait of two parents and their toddler in a garden. Right panel: the same baby's head-and-shoulders portrait on blush pink with space below for birthday text. No lettering was generated; the renderer adds editable text.

`google-g.svg` is the Google G asset from https://www.gstatic.com/firebasejs/ui/2.0.0/images/auth/google.svg, used as the requested corporate-cookie example.

The film artwork and three-row layout are defined separately from the packaging geometry, ready for real box-photo references.

Packaging geometry follows the white hinged box in local reference photos 1000001733.jpg through 1000001736.jpg (Downloads/box images). Original reference photos are not published. Window outline, depth, folding tabs, white crinkle filler and corner labels are modeled in order-packaging-box.mjs. The generated QR label links to the MBC homepage. The neutral oak serving table and props are illustrative. Film duration is 27 seconds, ending with the lid opening on the table.
