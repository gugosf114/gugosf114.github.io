Hand-Piped vs. Printed: Two Ways to Put Your Logo on a Cookie

My Baking Creations offers two distinct methods for corporate logo cookies. Both are made to order — no pre-made inventory, no off-the-shelf options. Every batch is custom.

Tier 1: Hand-Piped Royal Icing Cookies

Each cookie is individually piped by hand using royal icing. The result is a raised, textured surface with a glossy finish that catches light and photographs beautifully. The artisan quality is immediately apparent — these cookies feel like a gift, not a promotional item.

Best for:

  • Executive client gifts and deal-close thank-yous
  • Premium onboarding kits where first impressions matter
  • Brand moments where the handcrafted quality reflects on your company
  • Logos with clean lines, bold shapes, or strong iconography

Pricing starts at $6–$8 per cookie, reflecting the skilled hand labor involved in each piece.

Tier 2: Printed Edible Image Cookies

Your logo is printed in high resolution using food-safe edible ink onto a smooth royal icing base. This method captures fine gradients, photographic detail, and complex color palettes with precision — ideal when your brand identity requires exact reproduction.

Best for:

  • High-volume orders where consistency across hundreds of cookies matters
  • Logos with gradients, photography, or intricate multi-color detail
  • Conference giveaways, trade show booths, and bulk event distribution
  • Any order where per-unit cost efficiency is a priority

Pricing starts at $5–$7 per cookie.

How to choose: Hand-piped for prestige gifting and brand moments. Printed for high-volume orders or photo-realistic branding. When in doubt, share your logo and we'll recommend the right method — or show you both options side by side.

The minimum order for corporate logo cookies is one dozen (12 cookies). This is a studio-level, professional service — not a commodity product.

Ordering Corporate Logo Cookies: What to Expect

Step 1: Submit Your Inquiry

Start by submitting a request through the corporate order form or by emailing info@mybakingcreations.com. Include your event date, approximate quantity, and any logo files or brand guidelines you have available.

Step 2: Logo Review and Quote

Share your logo file (PNG, SVG, AI, or EPS preferred) along with your brand color codes (Pantone or hex). A custom quote is typically provided within 24 hours. For orders where you're unsure which method suits your logo, a digital proof showing both options can be prepared.

Step 3: Approval and Production

Once you approve the design and confirm the order, production begins. Standard turnaround is 1–2 weeks for most corporate orders. For large orders — 500 or more cookies — plan for 2–3 weeks to allow time for brand color matching and quality review across the full batch. Rush orders are available for established clients; contact the bakery directly to discuss timeline.

Step 4: Packaging and Delivery

Cookies can be:

  • Individually wrapped in clear cellophane bags — ideal for gift bags, swag kits, and event distribution
  • Arranged in presentation boxes — sets of 4, 6, or 12, suitable for client gifts and executive presentations
  • Displayed on platters for buffet-style event setups

Delivery covers the full Menlo Park and Palo Alto corridor, with reach across the Bay Area including Mountain View (94043), San Mateo (94401), South San Francisco (94080), and San Francisco. Pickup is available at the Daly City kitchen at 1096 Wildwood Ave (94015).

Payment options include invoice, purchase order (PO), credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Zelle. Itemized invoices are available for procurement teams.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order for corporate logo cookies in Menlo Park or Palo Alto?

The minimum order is one dozen (12 cookies). Most corporate clients in the Menlo Park and Palo Alto area order between 50 and 500 cookies per event, depending on the occasion and guest count.

Standard turnaround is 1–2 weeks. For orders over 500 cookies, plan for 2–3 weeks. If your event date is firm, earlier is always better — especially during busy seasons like Q4 holiday gifting and May conference season.
