Hand-Piped vs. Printed: Two Ways to Put Your Logo on a Cookie

My Baking Creations offers two distinct methods for corporate logo cookies. Both are made to order — no pre-made inventory, no off-the-shelf options. Every batch is custom.

Tier 1: Hand-Piped Royal Icing Cookies

Each cookie is individually piped by hand using royal icing. The result is a raised, textured surface with a glossy finish that catches light and photographs beautifully. The artisan quality is immediately apparent — these cookies feel like a gift, not a promotional item.

Best for:

  • Executive client gifts and deal-close thank-yous
  • Premium onboarding kits where first impressions matter
  • Brand moments where the handcrafted quality reflects on your company
  • Logos with clean lines, bold shapes, or strong iconography

Pricing starts at $6–$8 per cookie, reflecting the skilled hand labor involved in each piece.

Tier 2: Printed Edible Image Cookies

Your logo is printed in high resolution using food-safe edible ink onto a smooth royal icing base. This method captures fine gradients, photographic detail, and complex color palettes with precision — ideal when your brand identity requires exact reproduction.

Best for:

  • High-volume orders where consistency across hundreds of cookies matters
  • Logos with gradients, photography, or intricate multi-color detail
  • Conference giveaways, trade show booths, and bulk event distribution
  • Any order where per-unit cost efficiency is a priority

Pricing starts at $5–$7 per cookie.

How to choose: Hand-piped for prestige gifting and brand moments. Printed for high-volume orders or photo-realistic branding. When in doubt, share your logo and we'll recommend the right method — or show you both options side by side.

The minimum order for corporate logo cookies is one dozen (12 cookies). This is a studio-level, professional service — not a commodity product.
