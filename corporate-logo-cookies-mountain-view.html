The Ordering Process: From Logo File to Delivery

Getting branded cookies from My Baking Creations is straightforward. Here is how it works:

Step 1 — Submit your order request

Fill out the order request form at mybakingcreations.com/order-form or reach out via the corporate order page. Include your event date, approximate quantity, and any initial notes about your brand or theme.

Step 2 — Share your brand assets

Send your logo file (PNG, SVG, AI, or EPS preferred) along with your brand color codes. The more detail you provide upfront, the faster the proof process moves.

Step 3 — Review your design proof

Before production begins, you receive a digital mockup showing how your logo will appear on the cookie. Revisions are part of the process — nothing goes into production without your approval.

Step 4 — Production and delivery

Once approved, your cookies are baked fresh and decorated by hand. Plan ahead for large corporate events — earlier ordering gives more flexibility on timing and reduces pressure on both sides. Orders start from $150.

A note on lead time: We recommend reaching out as early as possible for large-scale corporate orders, conference giveaways, or events with firm deadlines. Rush availability depends on the current production schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you deliver directly to the Googleplex and other Mountain View tech campuses?

Yes. The Googleplex along Charleston Road and Amphitheatre Parkway is one of our most frequent delivery destinations. We deliver to specific building numbers and coordinate with your reception desk or facilities contact. LinkedIn, Intuit, and other Mountain View offices are also established delivery zones.

What is the minimum order for corporate logo cookies?

The minimum order is one dozen (12 cookies), with orders starting from $150. Most corporate clients order between 50 and 500 cookies depending on the event size and distribution plan.

How far in advance should I place a corporate cookie order?

We recommend reaching out as early as possible, particularly for large orders or events with firm deadlines. For standard orders under 100 pieces, two weeks is a comfortable lead time. For larger conference or company-wide orders, three to four weeks gives everyone more flexibility.

What logo file formats do you need?

PNG, SVG, AI, and EPS files all work well. A transparent background on your logo file produces the cleanest result. Include your brand color codes (hex or Pantone) when you submit.

Can different departments within the same company place separate orders?

Absolutely. This is one of the most common patterns among our corporate clients. Marketing, HR, executive assistants, and office managers each order independently for their own events and occasions. Every order is treated as a separate custom project.

Do you accept purchase orders and invoices?

Yes. My Baking Creations accepts purchase orders, invoices, credit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Zelle. W-9s and itemized invoices are available for procurement teams.

What is the difference between hand-piped and printed image cookies?

Hand-piped cookies are decorated entirely by hand using royal icing — every detail is applied freehand by our baker. They have an artisanal, textured quality and are the premium option for executive gifts and client presentations. Printed image cookies use high-resolution edible ink printing for pixel-perfect logo reproduction, which is efficient at scale and ideal for complex logos with fine detail or gradients. Both are available; the right choice depends on your logo and intended use.
