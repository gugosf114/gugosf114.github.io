Birthday Cake Styles We Create

A Note on Pricing

Custom birthday cakes at My Baking Creations start at $150. This is a premium, made-to-order product. Sculpted 3D cakes start at $300 for simpler designs and range to $650+ for complex multi-element sculptures. How to Order Your Custom Birthday Cake

Ordering is straightforward. Here's how it works:

1. Fill out the order request form at mybakingcreations.com/order-form. Share your event date, approximate guest count, theme ideas, and any inspiration photos you have.

2. Consult with Yana. Within 24–48 hours, you'll receive custom design sketches with pricing. There's no commitment at this stage — the sketch is part of the process. You review, request changes, and approve when the design is exactly right.

3. Approve and lock in your date. Once you approve the design, a deposit secures your spot on the production calendar. Your cake is then hand-crafted from scratch over the following days.

Lead time matters. We typically book 2–3 weeks in advance for custom cakes, and weekend dates fill quickly. For sculpted 3D cakes, 2–4 weeks is recommended. Order early — especially for Saturday celebrations.

Browse real photos before you order. Our gallery at mybakingcreations.com/gallery is searchable by keyword. Type "sneaker," "Mario," "floral," or "50th birthday" and see actual photos from past orders. No stock images, no renderings — only real cakes we've made.
