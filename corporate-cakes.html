Corporate Cakes, Cupcakes & Cake Pops for Bay Area Events

One Bakery, Every Corporate Dessert Format

My Baking Creations is a family-owned Bay Area bakery that has served corporate clients since 2012. Founded by Yana in Daly City (94015), the bakery operates as a full-service corporate dessert provider — handling everything from branded event cakes and individually portioned cupcakes to logo cake pops and gifting sets.

This page covers the three core formats corporate clients order most:

  • Decorated event cakes — milestone cakes, sculpted product replicas, and sheet cakes with company branding
  • Branded cupcakes — individually portioned, logo-topped, easy to distribute at any gathering size
  • Cake pops and cake balls — giftable, portable, and Pantone-matchable to your brand palette

All three formats can carry your company logo, brand colors, and custom messaging. Orders are fulfilled from the Daly City kitchen and delivered across the corporate corridor — SoMa (94103), the Financial District (94105), South San Francisco (94080), Mountain View (94043), and Menlo Park — as well as directly to corporate campuses throughout Silicon Valley and the Peninsula.

Clients include Google, Meta, Salesforce, DocuSign, PayPal, OpenAI, Stripe, Alaska Airlines, Twitch, Levi's, Kaiser Permanente, Cooley LLP, and the Golden State Warriors. Tech companies, fintech firms, and corporate event planners across the Bay Area rely on My Baking Creations for recurring internal events and one-time milestone moments alike.

Branded Event Cakes: From Milestone Moments to Product Launches

Corporate event cakes at My Baking Creations fall into what the bakery calls Tier 4 — sheet cakes and sculpted cakes designed specifically for company occasions. These are not generic celebration cakes with a name written in frosting. Every design is built around your brand.

Common use cases include:

  • Product launch celebrations with cakes featuring the new product's name, logo, or visual identity
  • Company anniversary milestones with custom fondant work and branded color schemes
  • Employee appreciation events and department offsites
  • Retirement send-offs with career-themed sculpted designs
  • Acquisition announcements and rebrand reveals

Cakes can be sculpted to match brand objects — a product replica, branded packaging shape, or company mascot — creating a high-impact visual centerpiece that photographs well and reinforces brand identity at the moment it matters. The Levi's jeans pocket cake created for an employee event is one example of how far this format can go.

Because departments within the same company often order independently — different floors, different events, different budgets — My Baking Creations is set up to handle repeat orders from the same organization without requiring a centralized procurement process. Tech and SaaS companies in SoMa (94103) and the Financial District (94105) are among the most frequent repeat clients for this reason.

Corporate event cakes are priced from $300 to $650+, depending on size, design complexity, and whether sculpting is involved.

Corporate Cupcakes: Individually Branded, Easy to Serve

Cupcakes solve a practical problem at corporate events: no cutting, no plates, no serving logistics. Each cupcake is a self-contained portion that guests can grab and go — which makes them ideal for large gatherings where a full cake would require coordination.

Customization options include:

  • Hand-piped logo toppers in royal icing matched to your brand palette
  • Branded color schemes across frosting and decoration
  • Custom messaging per batch — useful when different departments or events need distinct designs

Cupcakes work particularly well for:

  • All-hands meetings and town halls
  • Client appreciation events
  • Trade show booths and conference activations
  • Office happy hours and team celebrations

Cupcakes are priced from $5 to $8 each and are made to order — not pulled from a display case or sourced from a wholesale supplier. Every batch is hand-decorated at the Daly City kitchen.

Delivery reaches San Bruno (94066), Burlingame (94010), San Mateo (94401), and the Sunset District in San Francisco (94122), in addition to the full Bay Area corporate corridor. The minimum order is $150, which reflects the made-to-order, artisan nature of every product.

Cake Pops & Cake Balls: Branded Bites for Gifting and Events

Cake pops and cake balls are one of the most versatile formats in the corporate dessert lineup. They travel well, package cleanly, and carry branding effectively — making them a natural fit for situations where a full cake isn't practical.

Customization options:

  • Brand colors matched to your palette (Pantone or hex)
  • Logo-stamped chocolate coating or branded edible toppers
  • Custom packaging for individual gifting sets or bulk event distribution

Use cases where cake pops consistently perform:

  • Client gifting and thank-you packages
  • Swag bag additions for conferences and trade shows
  • New hire onboarding welcome kits
  • Multi-location office drops across Bay Area campuses

Because cake pops are individually wrapped and structurally compact, they're one of the easiest formats to distribute across multiple office locations in a single delivery run. The same logo-accuracy and brand-color standards that apply to full cakes and cupcakes apply here — these are not generic dipped cake balls with a ribbon.

Cake pops are priced from $5 to $10 each. Delivery covers the full Bay Area footprint, including Ingleside and Excelsior neighborhoods in San Francisco (94112) and the home base in Daly City (94015).

How to Order Corporate Desserts from My Baking Creations

The ordering process is straightforward and designed to work with how corporate teams actually operate.

Step 1 — Submit your inquiry

Use the order request form or email info@mybakingcreations.com. You can also call or text (415) 568-8060 during business hours (Monday–Saturday, 9am–6pm).

Step 2 — Share your event details and branding assets

Provide your event date, quantity needed, product format (cakes, cupcakes, cake pops, or a combination), and your logo file (PNG, SVG, AI, or EPS preferred) along with brand color codes (Pantone or hex). The more detail you share upfront, the faster the quote turnaround.

Step 3 — Receive your custom quote

Quotes are typically provided within 24 hours. For logo items, a digital proof is included so you can approve the design before production begins.

Lead time guidance:

  • Standard corporate orders: 1–2 weeks
  • Large orders (100+ pieces) or sculpted designs: 2–3 weeks minimum
  • Rush orders are available for established clients — contact the bakery directly to discuss timeline

All products are made to order by hand. There is no mass production, no frozen inventory, and no generic designs pulled from a template library. The minimum order is $150 — a reflection of the custom, artisan nature of every item that leaves the kitchen.

Payment options: Invoice and purchase orders (PO) accepted for corporate accounts. Credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Zelle also accepted.
