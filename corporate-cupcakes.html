Branded Cupcakes Built Around Your Company Identity

Corporate cupcakes from My Baking Creations are not off-the-shelf. Each cupcake is made to order and finished with either hand-piped royal icing or a custom printed edible topper that reproduces your logo, brand colors, or event theme with precision.

  • Hand-piped royal icing toppers — the same artisan technique used for My Baking Creations' hand-piped logo cookies, applied at the cupcake scale. Each topper is individually crafted, giving the finished product a premium, tactile quality that recipients notice immediately.
  • Printed edible image toppers — high-resolution edible ink printing that reproduces complex logos, gradients, and multi-color artwork with photographic accuracy. Ideal for companies with detailed brand guidelines or specific Pantone/hex color requirements.
  • Full color matching — from frosting tint to topper finish, every element of the cupcake is matched to your company's visual identity. If your brand uses a specific shade of blue or a particular gradient, that detail carries through to the final product.

My Baking Creations has produced branded cupcakes for tech companies, fintech firms, and Fortune 500 offices across San Francisco and the Peninsula — the same artisan quality and branding accuracy that goes into every corporate cookie order applies to every cupcake batch.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order for corporate cupcakes?

The minimum order is $150. For cupcakes, this typically covers a dozen or more depending on design complexity. Contact the bakery for a specific quote based on your quantity and topper style.

How far in advance should I order branded cupcakes for a corporate event?

My Baking Creations recommends submitting your order at least 7–10 business days before your event. For large orders or events with detailed brand specifications, two weeks gives the team time to match your exact colors and finalize the topper design. Rush orders are sometimes available — call (415) 568-8060 to check availability.

Yes. My Baking Creations matches brand colors using Pantone or hex codes for both frosting tints and edible image toppers. Provide your brand color codes when submitting your order and the team will confirm the match before production begins.

Do you deliver to offices in San Francisco and Silicon Valley?

Do you accept purchase orders or invoices for corporate accounts?

Yes. Invoice and purchase order (PO) payment is accepted for established corporate accounts. My Baking Creations also accepts credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Zelle.

Can different departments at the same company place separate orders?

Absolutely. My Baking Creations handles independent orders from different teams, floors, or departments within the same company. Each order is managed separately with its own timeline and specifications.
