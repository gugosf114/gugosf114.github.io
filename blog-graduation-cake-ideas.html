Graduation Cake Ideas 2026

Bay Area schools, colleges & universities

← Back
Scroll

Graduation season in the Bay Area runs from mid-May through June, and it's one of the most exciting times of the year for families celebrating their students' achievements. Whether your graduate is walking the stage at UC Berkeley, receiving their diploma from SF State, or finishing high school, the right cake turns the grad party into something truly memorable. Here's our complete guide to graduation cake ideas for 2026.

Popular Graduation Cake Designs

Graduation cakes range from elegant and understated to bold and school-spirited. These are the designs Bay Area families request most.

Cap & Gown Sculpted Cakes

The classic graduation cake — a 3D sculpted mortarboard (graduation cap) sitting atop a tiered cake. We sculpt the cap from fondant, complete with a tassel in the graduate's school color. Some families go further with a full cap-and-gown figure sculpted on top, personalized to match their graduate. These are true showpieces that get photographed from every angle.

School Mascot Cakes

Nothing shows school pride like a cake featuring the graduate's mascot. From the Cal Golden Bears to the SFSU Gators, the Stanford Cardinal to the Spartans of San Jose State, we've sculpted mascots for schools across the Bay Area. These cakes work especially well for student-athletes and anyone who bleeds their school colors.

Diploma Scroll Cakes

An elegant option that suits any school or degree. The cake is shaped and decorated to resemble an open diploma or rolled scroll, complete with the graduate's name, degree, and graduation year written in edible calligraphy. Tied with a fondant ribbon in school colors, it's a sophisticated choice that photographs beautifully.

Textbook Stack Cakes

Each tier is decorated to look like a different textbook from the graduate's major — perfect for the dedicated student. A nursing graduate might have anatomy and pharmacology "books," while a computer science grad might feature algorithm and data structures "textbooks." It's personal, creative, and always gets a laugh of recognition.

School Logo Cookies

Not every grad party needs a towering cake. Printed image cookies featuring the school's logo, mascot, or the graduate's face are a hit at any celebration. They're easy to display, easy to serve, and guests love taking one home as a keepsake. We can print any design on royal icing cookies using edible ink — just send us the image.

Bay Area Schools We Make Cakes For

We've made graduation cakes and cookies for students at schools across every corner of the Bay Area. Here's a look at the schools we serve most, organized by region.

San Francisco

  • San Francisco State University (SFSU) — Purple and gold Gator pride. Commencement typically held at the main campus quad.
  • University of San Francisco (USF) — Green and gold Dons. Ceremonies at St. Ignatius Church and War Memorial.
  • Academy of Art University — Creative graduates who appreciate artistic cake designs matching their portfolio aesthetic.

We deliver graduation cakes and cookies throughout San Francisco — from the Sunset to the Mission, SOMA to the Marina.

East Bay

  • UC Berkeley — Blue and gold Bears. One of the biggest graduation weekends in the Bay Area. Order early — Berkeley grad parties book fast.
  • Cal State East Bay — Red, black, and white Pioneers. Ceremonies at the Hayward campus and Concord campus.

We deliver to Berkeley, Hayward, Oakland, and surrounding East Bay cities.

South Bay

  • Stanford University — Cardinal red. Commencement in Stanford Stadium is one of the most photographed graduation events in the country.
  • San Jose State University (SJSU) — Blue and gold Spartans. The largest public university in the South Bay.
  • Santa Clara University — Bronco red. Intimate graduation ceremonies in the Mission Gardens.

We deliver to Palo Alto, San Jose, Santa Clara, and the entire South Bay corridor.

Peninsula

  • City College of San Francisco (CCSF) — Rams in blue and white. Multiple campus locations across SF.
  • Skyline College — Trojans in blue and white. Located in San Bruno, serving the entire Peninsula community college district.

We're based in Daly City, right in the heart of the Peninsula. Local delivery is fast and easy.

High School Graduations

We also make cakes for high school graduates across every Bay Area district — Westmoor, Jefferson, Lowell, Balboa, Lincoln, Carlmont, Sequoia, and dozens more. High school grad cakes are often more playful: think school-color sprinkles, "Class of 2026" fondant banners, and photo cookies of the graduate through the years.

Grad Party Dessert Packages

The best grad parties don't stop at cake. Combining multiple desserts creates a spread that feeds the crowd and gives everyone options. Here are our most popular graduation dessert combos.

The Classic Grad Package

  • 1 sculpted graduation cap cake (serves 20–30)
  • 2 dozen school-logo printed cookies
  • 1 dozen cake pops in school colors

Perfect for a backyard party with 30–50 guests. The cake is the centerpiece, the cookies double as favors, and the cake pops add color to the dessert table.

The Big Celebration Package

  • 1 tiered cake (2–3 tiers, serves 50–80)
  • 4 dozen custom cookies (mix of school logos and "Class of 2026" designs)
  • 2 dozen cake pops in school colors
  • 2 dozen cupcakes with graduation toppers

For larger parties at rented venues, restaurants, or community centers. Enough variety that every guest finds something they love.

The Cookie-Only Package

  • 3–5 dozen printed image cookies with the graduate's photo, school logo, or custom message

Ideal for office celebrations, teacher gifts, or sending a box to family members who can't attend in person. Cookies ship well and stay fresh for days.

When to Order Your Graduation Cake

Timing is everything during graduation season. Here's what you need to know to make sure your order is locked in.

Key Bay Area Graduation Dates (2026)

  • Mid-May: Stanford, Santa Clara University, some community colleges
  • Late May: UC Berkeley, SFSU, SJSU, USF, Cal State East Bay
  • Early–Mid June: Most Bay Area high schools

Order Timeline

For standard graduation cakes and cookies, 2–3 weeks before your party date is the sweet spot. This gives us time to design, bake, and perfect your order without rushing.

For sculpted cakes, large dessert packages, or orders during peak graduation weekends (UC Berkeley and SFSU commencement), 4 weeks ahead is strongly recommended. We cap our orders to maintain quality, and popular weekends do fill up.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order a graduation cake?

We recommend ordering 2 to 3 weeks before your ceremony date. May and June are our busiest months for graduation orders, so earlier is better. For sculpted cakes or large orders with cookies and cake pops, 4 weeks is ideal.

Can you put a school logo on a cake or cookies?

Yes! We can print school logos, mascots, and custom designs on cookies using edible ink printing. For cakes, we can recreate school logos in fondant or use edible image transfers. Just send us a high-resolution image of the logo and we'll handle the rest.

Do you offer graduation dessert packages with cakes and cookies together?

Absolutely! Our graduation party packages combine cakes, cookies, and cake pops at a better value than ordering each separately. A popular combo is a sculpted graduation cap cake, two dozen school-logo cookies, and a dozen cake pops in school colors. Request a quote and tell us your guest count for a custom package.
