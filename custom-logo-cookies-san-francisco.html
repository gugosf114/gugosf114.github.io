Hand-Piped vs. Printed Logo Cookies: Which Is Right for Your Order?

Every corporate logo cookie order starts with one decision: hand-piped royal icing or edible ink printing. Both methods are produced entirely in-house — nothing is outsourced — and both have been ordered by tech companies across SoMa (94103), the Financial District (94105), South San Francisco (94080), and the broader Peninsula.

Hand-Piped Royal Icing Cookies

Hand-piped cookies are decorated entirely by hand. Each cookie is individually piped and flooded with colored royal icing, recreating your logo freehand with a trained artist's touch.

  • The look: Artisan, slightly textured, with a glossy icing finish — no two cookies are exactly alike
  • Best for: Clean logos with bold lines, text-based marks, icons, and mascots; high-end client gifting; swag bags where craft and premium feel matter
  • Pricing: $6–$8 per cookie, reflecting the skilled hand labor involved
  • Lead time: Allow 2–3 weeks for larger orders; each cookie requires individual piping, flooding, and drying time
  • Ideal use cases: Executive client gifts, onboarding kits, brand moments where the artisan quality signals something about your company

Printed Edible Image Cookies

Printed cookies use food-safe edible ink printed onto edible paper, applied to a smooth royal icing base on a shortbread cookie. The result is a crisp, high-resolution reproduction of your logo.

  • The look: Smooth, clean, photo-quality — sharp edges, precise color, photographic accuracy
  • Best for: Complex multi-color logos, gradients, detailed illustrations, photo elements, or any design where exact brand color matching is critical
  • Pricing: $5–$7 per cookie; slightly more efficient at scale
  • Lead time: Typically 1–2 weeks for standard orders; faster production on large runs
  • Ideal use cases: Conference giveaways, trade show booth treats, bulk employee appreciation orders, any event requiring consistent reproduction across hundreds of cookies

Not sure which to choose? We create a digital proof showing how your logo translates to each method — you see both options before committing.
