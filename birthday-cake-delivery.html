How Our Cake Delivery Works: Lead Times & Scheduling

Ordering a custom birthday cake for delivery is straightforward, but timing matters. Here's what to expect:

Standard lead times:

  • Classic custom birthday cakes (decorated sheet cakes, character cakes, tiered designs): 1–2 weeks advance notice recommended
  • Sculpted 3D and hyper-realistic cakes: 2–4 weeks, depending on complexity — these require additional structural and design time
  • Larger corporate orders (multiple cakes or 100+ items): 3–4 weeks

How scheduling works:

1. Submit your order request through the order form at mybakingcreations.com/order-form

2. Choose your event date — delivery scheduling is confirmed as part of the order process

3. Receive a confirmed delivery window so you know exactly when to expect your cake — no guessing, no surprises

Worried about timing? Rush inquiries are sometimes possible depending on current production availability. The best way to ask is directly through the order form — we'll let you know what's feasible.

> We typically book 2–3 weeks in advance for custom orders. The earlier you reach out, the more design options are available to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance do I need to order a birthday cake for delivery in San Francisco?

We recommend 1–2 weeks for most custom birthday cakes. Sculpted and 3D cakes require 2–4 weeks. If your event is sooner, submit an inquiry through the order form and we'll let you know what's available.

Do you deliver to all San Francisco neighborhoods?

Yes. We deliver throughout all of San Francisco — from the Outer Sunset (94122) and Ingleside/Excelsior (94112) to SoMa (94103), the Financial District (94105), Pacific Heights, the Mission, and beyond. We also offer pickup at our Outer Sunset location at 1800 23rd Ave.

What is the minimum order for birthday cake delivery?

Our minimum order is $150. We do not offer budget or discount cakes — every order is a premium, custom, handcrafted product.

Can you deliver a birthday cake to an office building in SoMa or the Financial District?

Absolutely. We deliver to offices throughout SoMa and the Financial District regularly. For high-rise buildings, we coordinate with your reception desk and deliver to your floor. We recommend scheduling delivery at least 30 minutes before your event to account for lobby check-in and elevator time.

Do you deliver to South San Francisco and the Peninsula?

Yes. South San Francisco (94080) is just 10 minutes from our bakery. We also deliver to San Bruno (94066), Burlingame (94010), San Mateo (94401), Palo Alto (94301), and Mountain View (94043), among many other Peninsula cities.

Is the cake delivered by your team or a third-party courier?

Our team handles all deliveries directly. We do not use third-party couriers for cake delivery — this is how we ensure every cake, especially sculpted and tiered designs, arrives in perfect condition.
