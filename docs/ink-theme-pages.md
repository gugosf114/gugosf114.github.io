# Ink website theme

The Ink theme is explicitly enabled with `body.mbc-ink` on the pages below.
City and keyword SEO landing pages, WiM legal pages, and redirect stubs are excluded.

Shared assets: `site-ink-theme.css`, `site-ink-pages.css`, `site-showcase.js`.
Specialized form and studio colors: `site-ink-forms.css`. About-specific surfaces: `about-ink.css`.

The showcase retains each page's existing heading and carousel images. It advances every three seconds and has pause, arrows, dots, and phone swiping.
Order, upload, email, AI, and payment scripts are unchanged by the styling rollout.
