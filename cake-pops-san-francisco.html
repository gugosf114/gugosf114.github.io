Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between cake pops and cake balls?

Cake pops have a lollipop stick — easy to hold at standing receptions and trade show booths. Cake balls are the stickless version, typically presented in a box or on a tray. My Baking Creations produces both formats. The interior cake and coating are the same; the format depends on your event setup.

Can you match my company's brand colors?

Yes. Provide your Pantone or hex codes and we match the candy coating accordingly. For logo reproduction, we use edible image discs that deliver the same precision as our printed cookie line.

Do you deliver cake pops to corporate campuses?

We deliver throughout the San Francisco Bay Area, including SoMa (94103), the Financial District (94105), South San Francisco (94080), Mountain View (94043), Menlo Park, Palo Alto (94301), and the broader Peninsula corridor. Corporate campus delivery is a regular part of our operation.

How far in advance should I order?

Standard turnaround is 1–2 weeks for most orders. Large orders — particularly those over 500 pieces requiring precise brand color matching — benefit from two or more weeks of lead time. Contact us as early as possible to confirm availability for your event date.

Can I order cake pops alongside other items like cookies or a custom cake?

Absolutely. Many corporate and personal orders include a mix of cake pops, logo cookies, cupcakes, and a centerpiece cake — all coordinated to match your event theme or brand palette. We handle full dessert table packages for events of all sizes.

Is pickup available in San Francisco?

Yes. In addition to our main kitchen in Daly City (94015), pickup is available at our Outer Sunset location at 1800 23rd Ave, San Francisco, CA 94122.
