Logo Cupcakes That Represent Your Brand

Every cupcake My Baking Creations produces for a corporate event can be decorated with your company's exact logo, brand colors, or event theme. Two decoration methods are available depending on your design and quantity needs:

  • Hand-piped royal icing — Each cupcake is individually decorated by hand using royal icing matched to your brand palette. Best for bold logos, clean icons, and designs where the artisan quality is part of the impression.
  • Edible printed toppers — High-resolution edible ink printing reproduces your logo with photographic precision. Ideal for complex artwork, gradients, or large-quantity orders where consistency across every cupcake matters.

Both methods deliver the same result: a finished product that looks like it came from your marketing department, not a grocery store.

How to Order Branded Cupcakes for Your Office Event

The ordering process is straightforward and designed for busy corporate buyers:

1. Submit your order request — Use the order request form to share your event details, including date, quantity, and delivery address.

2. Share your logo and brand assets — High-resolution files in PNG, SVG, or PDF format produce the best results. Include your brand color codes (Pantone or hex) if exact matching is required.

3. Receive a custom quote — A quote is typically provided within 24 hours of your inquiry.

4. Confirm and schedule — Once you approve the quote and design direction, your order is locked in and production is scheduled.

5. Delivery or pickup — Direct delivery to your office or campus, or pickup from the Daly City kitchen (9 AM–6 PM, Monday–Saturday).

A few practical notes:

  • Lead time matters. Standard corporate orders benefit from at least 1–2 weeks of advance notice. Large-quantity orders or complex custom designs may require additional time. The earlier you reach out, the more flexibility there is on design and scheduling.
  • Logo files make a difference. A high-resolution source file — not a screenshot or low-res JPEG — ensures your logo reproduces cleanly on every cupcake.
  • Minimum orders reflect the artisan nature of the work. My Baking Creations does not produce generic or mass-market product. Every order is custom, and the minimum threshold reflects that commitment to quality.

Planning Corporate Cupcakes: What Office Managers Need to Know

If you're the person responsible for making the event happen, here's what you need to know before you place your order.

How far in advance should you order?

For standard corporate cupcake orders, 1–2 weeks is the recommended lead time. If your order involves a large quantity, a complex custom design, or a tight delivery window, reach out as early as possible. My Baking Creations works with recurring clients and can often streamline reorders for companies that host events regularly — if you're ordering for the third time this quarter, the process gets faster.

What information to have ready:

  • Event date and delivery time window
  • Delivery address, including building name and floor or suite number for campus deliveries
  • Estimated guest count
  • Logo files (PNG, SVG, or PDF preferred)
  • Brand color codes (Pantone or hex) if color accuracy is important
  • Any theme or color direction beyond the standard logo treatment

How many cupcakes to order:

A general planning rule: for a standard office gathering where cupcakes are the primary dessert, plan for 1–2 cupcakes per person. For events where other food is being served, 1 per person is typically sufficient. It's always worth ordering a small buffer — running out is a worse outcome than having a few extra.

Recurring events and reorders:

My Baking Creations works with companies that host frequent events — quarterly all-hands meetings, monthly birthday celebrations, recurring client appreciation programs. If your company falls into this category, mention it when you first reach out. Establishing a recurring relationship simplifies the process considerably for both sides.

The goal is simple: you place the order, we handle everything else, and the cupcakes arrive at your office looking exactly like your brand. Your team is impressed, your clients are impressed, and you look good — with zero stress on your end.

Frequently Asked Questions

My Baking Creations accepts corporate cupcake orders starting at one dozen (12 cupcakes). Most corporate clients order 50 to 200+ for office events. Contact us for pricing on your specific quantity and design.

Yes. Provide your Pantone or hex color codes and we'll match your brand palette as closely as possible. For edible printed toppers, color reproduction is highly accurate. For hand-piped royal icing, we mix custom colors to match your guidelines.
