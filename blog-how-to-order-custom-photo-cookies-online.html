Order Custom Cookies Online

Photo cookies for every occasion, logo cookies for company events

← Back
Scroll

You can now design your own cookies on our website. Put a photo, a company logo, or your own words on a fresh-baked cookie, see the exact print, and order in a few minutes. This guide shows how to order custom photo cookies and corporate logo cookies online, what they cost, and how fast they ship.

What You Can Put on a Cookie

Personalized cookies for all occasions

Birthdays, anniversaries, baby showers, graduations, weddings, thank-yous, congratulations, or just because. Use a face, a pet, a favorite moment, or an inside joke. You can start from one of 110 ready-made designs and change every word and color, or make your own from a blank cookie.

Corporate logo cookies for company events

Product launches, client gifts, conferences, team wins, and new-hire welcomes. Upload your company logo, pick your brand color, and see the print before you pay. Every cookie is wrapped on its own, so they are ready to hand out.

How to Order Custom Cookies Online, Step by Step

  1. Start. Open the cookie design page. Choose a ready-made design, make your own, or describe your idea and let the optional AI helper draw original artwork. You can also upload a photo right away (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 20 MB).
  2. Design. Change the words, the lettering, the colors, and the background. Add a photo and keep the whole picture, or remove the background so only your person, pet, or logo shows. Pick a square or a round cookie.
  3. Review. Check the crop and every edge. What you approve is the exact artwork we print on your cookies.
  4. Delivery. Choose how many cookies you want. Ship by FedEx or pick up in Daly City. Enter your ZIP code to see the price and the arrival date for FedEx Ground, 2Day, and Standard Overnight. We ship within 48 hours, or you can choose your own ship date.
  5. Pay. Pay securely by card or PayPal. Your approved design goes straight to our bakery.

Price, Size, and Packaging

  • $5 per cookie, 12-cookie minimum, so a dozen is $60
  • Each different design needs at least 12 cookies
  • 3-inch cookies, square or round, vanilla flavor
  • Gift box and individual wrapping included
  • Baked in Daly City, California
  • Shipping is the FedEx price for your ZIP code

Tips for a Good Print

A clear, bright photo prints best. Short messages read best on a 3-inch cookie, and longer messages shrink to fit inside it. Screen colors and edible inks can look slightly different, so review your design carefully before you approve it.

Bigger Company Orders and Hand-Piped Cookies

Planning cookies for a large company event, or want hand-piped royal icing instead of a print? Send us a corporate order request, read our guide to custom logo cookies, or browse the corporate cookie gallery and the custom cookie gallery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you print our company logo on cookies?

Yes. Upload your logo as a PNG, JPG or WebP, pick your brand color, and see the exact print before you pay. Corporate logo cookies come wrapped one by one, so they are easy to hand out at company events. You can also check out our corporate order request page.

Can you put a photo on a cookie?

Yes. We print your photo in edible ink on a fresh-baked cookie with white royal icing. Keep the whole photo, or remove the background so only your person or pet shows.

How much do custom cookies cost?

$5 per cookie with a 12-cookie minimum, so a dozen is $60. The gift box and individual wrapping are included. Shipping is the FedEx price for your ZIP code.

How fast do custom cookies ship?

We bake and ship within 48 hours, or you can choose your own ship date. Pick FedEx Ground, 2Day or Standard Overnight at checkout and see the arrival date before you pay. Pickup in Daly City is free.

Do you ship outside the San Francisco Bay Area?

Yes. We ship across the United States by FedEx. For Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico we send you a shipping quote first.

What size and flavor are the cookies?

Each cookie is 3 inches, square or round, and vanilla flavored. The print is edible and safe to eat.

Ready to Say It on a Cookie?

Design custom photo cookies or corporate logo cookies online and see the exact print before you order. $60 a dozen.
