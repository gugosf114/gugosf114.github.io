Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you visit mybakingcreations.com or place an order, we may collect:

  • Contact information: name, email address, phone number, and delivery address when you place an order or submit an inquiry.
  • Order details: cake/dessert preferences, event dates, and special requests.
  • Usage data: pages visited, time spent on site, and referring pages, collected via Google Analytics.
  • Device information: browser type, operating system, and screen size for website optimization.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill your bakery orders.
  • Communicate with you about your order status, consultations, and inquiries.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Send occasional updates or promotions (only if you opt in).

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share limited information with:

  • Service providers: payment processors, delivery services, and email platforms that help us operate our business.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics to understand website usage (anonymized data).
  • Legal requirements: if required by law or to protect our rights.

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience. These include:

  • Essential cookies: required for the website to function properly.
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our site.

You can control cookies through your browser settings.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.
  • Request that we stop processing your data.

California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
