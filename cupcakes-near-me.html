How to Order Custom Cupcakes

Ordering from My Baking Creations is straightforward. Every order begins with an inquiry — not a walk-in, not a same-day request.

Here's how the process works:

1. Submit your order request via the order form or the corporate quote form for business orders

2. Share your event details — occasion, event date, quantity needed, and any design vision you have (colors, theme, logo files for corporate orders)

3. Receive a custom quote — typically within 24 hours for corporate inquiries

4. Approve the design — for branded orders, we confirm decoration details before production begins

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get cupcakes with my company logo on them?

Yes. We offer two decoration methods for corporate cupcakes: hand-piped royal icing (ideal for bold, clean logos and artisan presentation) and edible printed toppers (ideal for complex artwork and large batches requiring consistent reproduction). Both are available for Bay Area delivery or pickup.

Do different departments at the same company order separately?

Absolutely. It's one of the most common patterns we see. Teams at Google, Meta, Salesforce, and other major companies place independent orders for their own events — product launches, team celebrations, onboarding kits — without coordinating through a single point of contact.

How far in advance should I order cupcakes?

We recommend at least one to two weeks for most cupcake orders. For large corporate batches — especially those requiring brand color matching across hundreds of pieces — two weeks gives our team the time to execute every detail accurately. Rush orders may be available; call us to discuss your timeline.

Do you deliver to offices in SoMa or the Financial District?

Yes. We deliver regularly to corporate offices in SoMa (94103) and the Financial District (94105), as well as campuses in South San Francisco (94080), Menlo Park, Mountain View, and across the Peninsula.

Can I combine cupcakes with a cookie or cake order?

Yes. Many of our corporate and personal orders include a mix of cupcakes, logo cookies, cake pops, and a centerpiece cake — all coordinated to the same theme or brand palette. Let us know what you're envisioning and we'll put together a custom quote.

Where can I pick up my order?

Pickup is available at our main kitchen at 1096 Wildwood Ave, Daly City, CA 94015, or at our Outer Sunset SF location at 1800 23rd Ave, San Francisco, CA 94122.
