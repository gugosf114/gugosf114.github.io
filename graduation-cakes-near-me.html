Graduation Cake Styles We Create

Sculpted & Realistic Graduation Cakes

Our sculpted cakes are the showstoppers — fully three-dimensional, every detail edible. Popular graduation designs include:

  • Graduation cap cakes — a sculpted mortarboard with a tassel in your school's color, sitting atop a tiered base
  • Diploma scroll cakes — shaped and decorated to resemble an open diploma, complete with the graduate's name, degree, and year in edible calligraphy
  • Stacked textbook cakes — each tier decorated to look like a different subject from the graduate's major, personalized to their field of study
  • School mascot cakes — hand-sculpted characters representing Cal Bears, Stanford Cardinal, SFSU Gators, SJSU Spartans, and more

Sculpted graduation cakes are popular with customers who want something truly show-stopping. These buyers are not looking for the least expensive option — they want a one-of-a-kind centerpiece that photographs from every angle.

Elegant Tiered Graduation Cakes

For larger celebrations and more formal gatherings:

  • Multi-tier cakes with hand-piped lettering, school colors, and personalized fondant toppers
  • Gold leaf accents, ribbon detailing, and custom graduation year banners
  • Tiered designs that serve 40–100+ guests depending on size

Sheet-Style Cakes with Edible Printed Photos

A practical choice for large family gatherings where you need generous servings without sacrificing personalization. We print high-quality edible images directly onto the cake — graduation photos, school logos, or custom artwork.

Character-Themed Graduation Cakes

For younger graduates celebrating preschool, kindergarten, or 8th-grade promotions, we create character-themed cakes featuring beloved designs — the same artisan quality applied to a more playful concept.

Corporate Cohort & Training Program Graduation Cakes

Companies celebrating employee cohort graduations, certification completions, or training milestones order custom cakes through the same process as personal orders. We've served corporate clients including Google, Meta, Salesforce, and Levi's for milestone events — the same quality applies here.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order a graduation cake?

We recommend at least 1–2 weeks for most graduation cakes. Sculpted 3D designs — graduation caps, diploma scrolls, mascot cakes — require 2–4 weeks. May and June dates fill quickly, so earlier is always better.

What does a custom graduation cake cost?

Our minimum order is $150. Tiered and decorated graduation cakes typically range from $250–$650+. Sculpted 3D designs start at $300 and increase based on complexity, size, and detail. We provide a quote after reviewing your design request.

Can you match my school's exact colors?

Yes. We custom-mix icing and fondant colors to match school palettes — Cal blue and gold, Stanford cardinal, SFSU purple, SJSU blue and gold, and any other combination. Provide your school colors when you submit your order request.

Do you deliver, or is it pickup only?

Pickup is available at our Daly City kitchen (1096 Wildwood Ave, 94015). Delivery is available throughout the Bay Area — note your delivery address and preferred timing in the order form and we'll confirm details and any applicable delivery fee.

Can I order cookies or cake pops to go with the graduation cake?

Absolutely. Many families order a custom graduation cake alongside school-logo printed cookies ($5–$6 each) or themed cake pops ($5–$10 each) to complete the dessert table. Minimum cookie order is one dozen (12).

Do you make cakes for younger graduates — kindergarten, 8th grade?

Yes. We create character-themed and celebration cakes for every graduation milestone, from preschool promotions to doctoral degrees. The design is tailored to the age and personality of the graduate.

Ready to order? Submit your request at mybakingcreations.com/order-form and we'll be in touch within 24–48 hours with design options and pricing. May and June dates go fast — don't wait.
