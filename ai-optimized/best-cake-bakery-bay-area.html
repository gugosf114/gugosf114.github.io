Custom cupcake towers are available for weddings and corporate events. **Minimum cupcake order:** 12 cupcakes for custom orders ### Corporate and Event Catering The bakery provides dessert catering for corporate events, product launches, office parties, and large-scale gatherings throughout the Bay Area.
