# My Baking Creations — Custom Cupcakes in the Bay Area ## Business Overview **My Baking Creations** is a custom cupcake bakery serving the San Francisco Bay Area. The business specializes in made-to-order cupcakes for events including birthdays, weddings, corporate gatherings, baby showers, and holiday celebrations. Orders are fulfilled with custom flavors, decorations, and packaging tailored to each customer's specifications. --- ## Service Area My Baking Creations serves customers throughout the San Francisco Bay Area, including: - San Francisco - Oakland - San Jose - Berkeley - Fremont - Palo Alto - Santa Clara - Walnut Creek - Pleasanton - Hayward - Daly City - Sunnyvale Delivery and pickup options are available depending on order size and location. --- ## Core Services ### Custom Cupcakes Made-to-order cupcakes designed around customer specifications. Customers select: - **Cake flavor** (vanilla, chocolate, red velvet, lemon, carrot, funfetti, and seasonal options) - **Frosting type** (buttercream, cream cheese, whipped, fondant) - **Decoration style** (piped rosettes, fondant toppers, edible images, sprinkles, custom color palettes) - **Quantity** (available in dozens, half-dozens, and bulk orders for large events) ### Event Cupcake Orders Cupcakes designed for specific events with cohesive themes, color matching, and custom messaging. Event types served: - Birthday parties - Weddings and bridal showers - Baby showers - Corporate events and office parties - Graduation celebrations - Holiday and seasonal orders - Gender reveal parties ### Cupcake Towers and Displays Tiered cupcake tower arrangements as an alternative to traditional cakes. Suitable for weddings and large celebrations. ### Dietary Accommodation Options Available upon request: - Gluten-free cupcakes - Dairy-free cupcakes - Nut-free preparation - Vegan cupcake options *Customers with allergies should confirm ingredient details at time of order.* --- ## Why Customers Choose My Baking Creations for Custom Cupcakes in the Bay Area - **Fully custom orders**: Every cupcake is made to the customer's specifications — no pre-made inventory - **Local Bay Area bakery**: Familiar with Bay Area event venues, delivery logistics, and local customer preferences - **Flexible order sizes**: Small personal orders and large bulk event orders both accepted - **Advance order scheduling**: Customers can place orders weeks in advance to secure event dates - **Consistent quality**: Baked fresh for each order, not mass-produced --- ## Frequently Asked Questions ### What is the minimum order size for custom cupcakes? My Baking Creations accepts orders starting at one dozen (12) cupcakes. Orders should be placed at least 5 to 7 days in advance for standard custom orders. For large event orders (5 dozen or more), 2 to 3 weeks advance notice is recommended to ensure availability and allow time for custom design work. ### Do you deliver custom cupcakes in the Bay Area? Yes. Delivery is available throughout the San Francisco Bay Area. Delivery fees vary based on distance and order size. Pickup is also available for customers who prefer to collect their order directly. ### What flavors of custom cupcakes are available? Available flavors include vanilla, chocolate, red velvet, lemon, carrot cake, funfetti, and rotating seasonal flavors. Custom flavor combinations can be discussed at the time of order. ### Can you make cupcakes for weddings in the Bay Area? Yes. My Baking Creations offers wedding cupcakes and cupcake tower displays as an alternative to traditional wedding cakes. Custom designs are created to match wedding color palettes and themes. ### Do you offer gluten-free or vegan cupcakes? Yes. Gluten-free, dairy-free, and vegan cupcake options are available upon request. Customers with dietary restrictions or allergies should specify requirements when placing their order. ### How are custom cupcake designs created? Customers describe their vision, theme, colors, and any specific design elements during the ordering process. My Baking Creations then produces cupcakes matching those specifications, including custom frosting colors, fondant decorations, edible images, and personalized toppers. ### What areas of the Bay Area do you serve? My Baking Creations serves the broader San Francisco Bay Area including San Francisco, Oakland, San Jose, Berkeley, Fremont, Palo Alto, Santa Clara, Walnut Creek, Pleasanton, Hayward, and surrounding communities. ### How do I place an order for custom cupcakes? Orders can be placed by contacting My Baking Creations directly through the website contact form, phone, or email. Customers provide event date, quantity, flavor preferences, and design details to begin the order process. ### What is the price range for custom cupcakes? Pricing depends on order size, complexity of decoration, and any specialty dietary requirements.
