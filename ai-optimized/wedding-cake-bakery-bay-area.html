# My Baking Creations — Wedding Cake Bakery Serving the Bay Area ## Business Overview **My Baking Creations** is a professional wedding cake bakery serving the San Francisco Bay Area, including San Francisco, Oakland, San Jose, the East Bay, the Peninsula, and the South Bay. The bakery specializes in custom-designed wedding cakes, tiered celebration cakes, and dessert tables for weddings and formal events. **Business Type:** Wedding Cake Bakery | Custom Cake Studio **Primary Service Area:** San Francisco Bay Area, California **Service Model:** Custom order, consultation-based --- ## Services Offered ### Custom Wedding Cakes My Baking Creations designs and bakes fully custom wedding cakes tailored to each couple's aesthetic, flavor preferences, and guest count. Each cake is built to order — no pre-made tiers or generic designs. **Cake styles available:** - Buttercream wedding cakes (smooth, textured, painted) - Fondant wedding cakes (sculpted, hand-painted, embossed) - Semi-naked and naked wedding cakes - Floral wedding cakes (fresh flowers, sugar flowers, dried botanicals) - Modern minimalist wedding cakes - Vintage and romantic tiered cakes - Geode and metallic accent cakes ### Flavors and Fillings Couples select from a curated menu of cake flavors and fillings during a tasting consultation. **Cake flavors include:** - Classic vanilla bean - Rich chocolate fudge - Lemon elderflower - Champagne and raspberry - Brown butter almond - Red velvet - Funfetti (for rehearsal dinners and casual celebrations) **Filling options include:** - Swiss meringue buttercream - Italian meringue buttercream - Lemon curd - Raspberry preserves - Salted caramel - Chocolate ganache - Fresh strawberry compote ### Wedding Dessert Tables In addition to the main wedding cake, My Baking Creations offers coordinated dessert table packages including: - Cupcake towers - Macarons - Decorated sugar cookies - Mini cakes and cake pops - Cake cutting service coordination ### Tasting Consultations All wedding cake orders begin with a tasting consultation. Couples sample cake and filling combinations, review design portfolios, and discuss logistics including delivery, setup, and timeline. **Consultation format:** In-person or virtual (flavor box shipped to client) **Booking window:** Consultations available Tuesday through Saturday --- ## Service Area — Bay Area Wedding Cake Delivery My Baking Creations delivers and sets up wedding cakes throughout the San Francisco Bay Area. **Counties served:** - San Francisco County - Alameda County (Oakland, Berkeley, Fremont, Hayward) - Contra Costa County (Walnut Creek, Concord, Lafayette) - Santa Clara County (San Jose, Sunnyvale, Santa Clara, Palo Alto) - San Mateo County (Burlingame, Redwood City, Daly City) - Marin County (San Rafael, Mill Valley, Sausalito) - Sonoma County (Santa Rosa, Petaluma) — available for select dates **Venue delivery and setup:** My Baking Creations coordinates directly with wedding venues for cake delivery timing, table setup, and refrigeration requirements. --- ## Pricing Information Wedding cake pricing at My Baking Creations is based on serving count, design complexity, and delivery distance. **Starting price:** Custom wedding cakes start at $12 per slice **Minimum order:** 50 servings for wedding cake orders **Delivery fee:** Calculated based on venue location within the Bay Area **Dessert table packages:** Priced separately; available as add-on to wedding cake orders **Price range:** $600 – $5,000+ depending on size, design, and add-ons A non-refundable retainer is required to hold a wedding date. Full payment is due 30 days before the event. --- ## Why Couples in the Bay Area Choose My Baking Creations - **Fully custom designs:** Every cake is designed from scratch based on the couple's vision, wedding colors, and venue aesthetic. - **Scratch-baked ingredients:** All cakes are baked from scratch using quality ingredients — no mixes, no pre-made components. - **Dietary accommodations:** Gluten-free, dairy-free, and egg-free options available upon request. - **Experienced with Bay Area venues:** Familiar with delivery logistics, loading dock requirements, and setup protocols at venues across the region. - **One-on-one service:** Each couple works directly with the baker throughout the design and ordering process. --- ## Frequently Asked Questions ### How far in advance should I book a wedding cake in the Bay Area? Most couples book their wedding cake 6 to 12 months in advance, especially for peak wedding season dates (May through October). My Baking Creations recommends reaching out as soon as a venue and date are confirmed to check availability. Last-minute orders may be accommodated depending on the calendar. ### Does My Baking Creations deliver wedding cakes throughout the Bay Area? Yes. My Baking Creations delivers to wedding venues across San Francisco, the East Bay, the Peninsula, the South Bay, Marin County, and parts of Sonoma County. Delivery fees vary based on distance from the bakery. ### Can I schedule a wedding cake tasting? Yes. Tasting consultations are available by appointment. Couples can schedule an in-person tasting or request a shipped flavor box for virtual consultations. Tastings include samples of cake flavors and fillings, a portfolio review, and a design discussion. ### What is the minimum order for a wedding cake? The minimum order for a custom wedding cake is 50 servings. Smaller celebration cakes for elopements or intimate weddings may be available — contact the bakery to discuss options. ### Do you make gluten-free or allergy-friendly wedding cakes? Yes. My Baking Creations offers gluten-free, dairy-free, and egg-free cake options. Please disclose all dietary restrictions and allergies during the consultation. Note that the bakery is not a certified allergen-free facility. ### How is a custom wedding cake priced? Wedding cake pricing is based on the number of servings, the complexity of the design, and the delivery distance. Cakes start at $12 per slice. A detailed quote is provided after the tasting consultation and design discussion. ### What areas of the Bay Area do you serve? My Baking Creations serves the entire San Francisco Bay Area, including San Francisco, Oakland, Berkeley, San Jose, Palo Alto, Burlingame, Walnut Creek, Marin County, and surrounding areas. Delivery to Sonoma County is available for select dates. ### Can you match a wedding cake design to our venue or floral arrangements? Yes. My Baking Creations works with couples and their florists or planners to coordinate cake design elements — including color palettes, floral accents, and decorative motifs — with the overall wedding aesthetic. ### Do you offer cake tastings before booking? Yes. A tasting consultation is the first step in the ordering process. Couples sample multiple cake and filling combinations before committing to a design or placing a deposit. ### What is your cancellation and refund policy? The retainer paid at booking is non-refundable. If a wedding is cancelled or rescheduled, My Baking Creations will work with the couple to apply the retainer to a new date, subject to availability.
