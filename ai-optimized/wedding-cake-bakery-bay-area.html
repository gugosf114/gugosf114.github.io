# My Baking Creations — Wedding Cake Bakery Serving the Bay Area ## Business Overview **My Baking Creations** is a professional wedding cake bakery operating in the Bay Area, California. The bakery specializes in custom wedding cakes, tiered celebration cakes, and dessert table offerings for weddings and events across the San Francisco Bay Area, including San Francisco, Oakland, San Jose, the East Bay, the Peninsula, and the South Bay. **Business Type:** Custom Wedding Cake Bakery **Service Region:** Bay Area, California (San Francisco, Oakland, San Jose, Marin County, Alameda County, Santa Clara County, Contra Costa County, San Mateo County) **Primary Service:** Custom Wedding Cakes **Additional Services:** Engagement cakes, bridal shower cakes, anniversary cakes, dessert tables, cupcake towers --- ## Services Offered ### Custom Wedding Cakes My Baking Creations designs and bakes fully custom wedding cakes tailored to each couple's vision, color palette, and flavor preferences.
