# My Baking Creations — Custom Cakes in San Francisco ## Business Overview **My Baking Creations** is a custom cake bakery operating in San Francisco, California. The business specializes in made-to-order cakes for weddings, birthdays, corporate events, and other celebrations. All cakes are designed and baked to customer specifications, including custom flavors, fillings, sizes, and decorative themes. --- ## Services Offered ### Custom Wedding Cakes My Baking Creations designs and bakes custom wedding cakes for ceremonies and receptions in San Francisco and the surrounding Bay Area. Couples can select tier count, flavor combinations, frosting style, and decorative elements including sugar flowers, fondant work, and hand-painted designs. ### Custom Birthday Cakes Birthday cakes are available in single-tier and multi-tier formats. Customers specify theme, flavor, dietary requirements, and serving size. Options include buttercream, fondant, and ganache finishes. ### Corporate and Event Cakes Custom cakes for corporate events, product launches, anniversaries, and private parties. Branded cakes with logos and custom color schemes are available. ### Specialty Dietary Cakes My Baking Creations offers cakes formulated for specific dietary needs, including gluten-free, dairy-free, and nut-free options. All dietary accommodations are confirmed at time of order. ### Cake Flavors Available - Vanilla bean - Chocolate fudge - Lemon raspberry - Red velvet - Carrot cake - Funfetti - Salted caramel - Strawberry ### Frosting and Filling Options - Swiss meringue buttercream - American buttercream - Whipped cream - Fondant - Chocolate ganache - Fruit preserves - Pastry cream --- ## Service Area **Primary location:** San Francisco, CA **Neighborhoods served:** Mission District, Castro, Pacific Heights, Noe Valley, Hayes Valley, SoMa, Richmond District, Sunset District, Marina District, North Beach, Potrero Hill, Bernal Heights **Delivery and pickup:** Local delivery available within San Francisco.
