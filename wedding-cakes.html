Custom Wedding Cakes Handcrafted in the San Francisco Bay Area

Artisan Wedding Cakes Made to Order in Daly City & Beyond

My Baking Creations is a family-owned custom bakery founded in 2012 by Yana, a professional baker and cake designer based in Daly City (94015). Every wedding cake that leaves the kitchen is made entirely from scratch, by hand, to order — no two are identical, no templates, no shortcuts.

This is not a chain bakery or a grocery-store cake counter. It is a boutique operation where every detail of your wedding cake is discussed directly with the baker before a single layer is baked. Couples across San Francisco, South San Francisco (94080), San Bruno (94066), the Sunset District (94122), Ingleside (94112), Burlingame (94010), and San Mateo (94401) have trusted My Baking Creations to create the centerpiece of their most important day.

If you want a wedding cake that is genuinely custom — designed around your vision, your flavors, and your celebration — this is where to start.

Wedding Cake Styles: From Classic Tiers to Sculpted Showpieces

My Baking Creations offers a full range of wedding cake styles, from timeless elegance to jaw-dropping sculptural centerpieces.

Classic Tiered Wedding Cakes

Multi-tier buttercream and fondant-finished cakes remain the most popular choice for Bay Area weddings. Smooth finishes, textured buttercream, and clean lines suit everything from intimate City Hall ceremonies to large reception venues across the Peninsula and Marin County.

Floral & Hand-Decorated Designs

Fresh floral accents, sugar flowers, gold leaf, and hand-piped royal icing detail work are all available. Monograms, custom cake toppers, and personalized inscriptions can be incorporated into any design.

Minimalist Modern Cakes

For couples who prefer understated elegance — naked cakes, semi-naked styles, or geometric fondant designs — Yana's portfolio includes refined, contemporary options that photograph beautifully.

Sculpted & Hyper-Realistic Showpieces

My Baking Creations is known throughout the Bay Area for hyper-realistic, sculpted cake designs. Couples who want a true conversation piece — a cake that looks like an object, a scene, or something entirely unexpected — can commission a fully sculpted wedding centerpiece. Every detail is edible. This is the same sculpting expertise behind cakes like a Ferrari for a car collector and a full sushi platter, applied to your wedding vision.

Wedding Cake Flavors & Tasting Consultations

Choosing your wedding cake flavor is part of the experience. My Baking Creations offers a complimentary tasting and design consultation for every wedding cake order — available in person at the Daly City kitchen or via a shipped tasting box for couples who cannot visit in person.

Available Flavors Include:

  • Vanilla Bean — classic, crowd-pleasing, pairs with nearly any filling
  • Chocolate Ganache — rich and indulgent, popular for fall and winter weddings
  • Lavender Honey — floral and sophisticated, ideal for garden and outdoor receptions
  • Lemon Elderflower — bright and refreshing, a natural fit for spring and summer celebrations
  • Guava Chiffon — light and tropical, a Bay Area favorite
  • Strawberry Vanilla Cream — fresh and universally loved

Multi-tier cakes can feature different flavors on each tier, giving guests variety and ensuring everyone finds something they love. Fillings, frostings, and flavor combinations are all confirmed during the consultation — nothing is left to guesswork.

The consultation is also where design details are finalized: tier count, topper style, color palette, floral elements, and any custom decorative work. Yana works directly with each couple to ensure the finished cake matches the vision discussed, not a generic interpretation of it.

How to Order Your Wedding Cake from My Baking Creations

The ordering process is straightforward and designed to give couples a bespoke experience from the first conversation to the final delivery.

Step-by-Step:

1. Browse the gallery — Review real completed wedding cakes at mybakingcreations.com/gallery-cakes-wedding to identify styles, tiers, and design directions that resonate with you.

2. Submit your order request — Complete the form at mybakingcreations.com/order-form with your event date, approximate guest count, and any design ideas or inspiration images.

3. Consultation & design — Yana reviews your request and follows up within 24–48 hours. Design sketches, flavor selections, and pricing are discussed directly — no middlemen, no automated responses.

4. Approval & scheduling — Once the design is confirmed and a deposit is placed, your date is locked in and production is scheduled.

5. Handcrafted creation — Your cake is made from scratch over the days leading up to your event.

6. Delivery or pickup — Pickup is available at the Daly City kitchen (1096 Wildwood Ave, 94015) or the Outer Sunset SF location (1800 23rd Ave, 94122). Delivery is available throughout the Bay Area, including South San Francisco (94080), San Bruno (94066), Ingleside/Excelsior (94112), Burlingame (94010), San Mateo (94401), and beyond.

Important Notes:

  • Wedding cakes are a premium custom service. Pricing is based on tier count, serving size, design complexity, and decorative elements — inquire directly for a custom quote.
  • Lead time matters. Peak wedding season in the Bay Area runs May through October. Booking 3–6 months in advance is strongly recommended for popular dates. For simpler designs, 6–8 weeks is typically sufficient.
  • Delivery is included for venues within 30 miles of the Daly City kitchen. Delivery to Napa Valley, Marin County, and other extended areas is available — contact the bakery for details.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book my wedding cake?

For peak season (May–October), booking 3–6 months in advance is recommended. For simpler designs or off-peak dates, 6–8 weeks is usually sufficient. Contact the bakery as early as possible to confirm availability for your date.

Is a tasting included?

Yes. Every wedding cake order includes a complimentary tasting. Tastings are available in person at the Daly City kitchen or via a shipped tasting box for couples outside the immediate area.

Do you deliver to wedding venues across the Bay Area?

Yes. My Baking Creations delivers and sets up wedding cakes at venues throughout San Francisco, the Peninsula, East Bay, South Bay, and North Bay including Marin County and Napa Valley. Delivery is included within 30 miles of the Daly City kitchen.

Can I have different flavors on each tier?

Absolutely. Different flavors per tier are very popular and are confirmed during your consultation. Up to three flavors per tier are available.

How is pricing determined?

Wedding cake pricing depends on tier count, number of servings, design complexity, fondant work, sugar flowers, custom toppers, and decorative elements. There is no published price list because every cake is custom — submit an order request and Yana will provide a quote based on your specific vision.

Can you make a sculpted or hyper-realistic wedding cake?

Yes. My Baking Creations specializes in sculpted and hyper-realistic cake designs. If you want a wedding cake that doubles as a true showpiece — shaped like an object, a scene, or something entirely unexpected — this is a genuine specialty of the bakery.
