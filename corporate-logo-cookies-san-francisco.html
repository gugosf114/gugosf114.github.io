Hand-Piped and Printed Logo Cookies Built for Bay Area Brands

When a San Francisco tech company orders branded cookies, the goal is never just dessert. It's a brand impression — something that gets photographed, shared on Slack, and remembered. My Baking Creations produces corporate logo cookies using two distinct methods, each suited to different use cases and brand requirements.

Hand-Piped Royal Icing Cookies

Every stroke is applied by hand using royal icing. The result is an artisan, slightly textured finish with a glossy surface that catches light — the kind of detail that signals premium quality the moment someone picks one up. Hand-piped cookies are ideal for:

  • Simple, bold logos with clean lines and strong color contrast
  • Text-based marks and wordmarks in bold fonts
  • Executive client gifts where the handcrafted quality is part of the message
  • Smaller runs where the artisan character is worth the additional per-unit investment

Pricing starts at $6–$8 per cookie, reflecting the skilled hand labor involved.

Edible-Ink Printed Cookies

Your logo file is printed in food-safe edible ink onto edible paper, then applied to a smooth royal icing base on a shortbread cookie. The result is photographic-quality reproduction — sharp edges, precise color matching, and the ability to handle gradients, detailed illustrations, and multi-color artwork that would be impractical to pipe by hand.

Printed cookies are the right choice for:

  • Complex logos with fine detail or multiple colors
  • Large-volume orders where consistency across hundreds of cookies matters
  • Brand assets that require exact color accuracy

Pricing starts at $5–$7 per cookie, and production scales efficiently for bulk runs.

Every cookie — hand-piped or printed — is made to order at our Daly City studio. Nothing is mass-produced, nothing is pulled from frozen inventory. The Right Cookie for Every Corporate Use Case

San Francisco's tech industry has developed a distinct culture around branded treats. Here are the scenarios My Baking Creations handles most often for corporate clients:

Product Launches

Printed cookies featuring app icons, product screenshots, or campaign branding travel well, photograph beautifully, and give the launch team something tangible to celebrate with. They double as social media content — the kind of detail that ends up in a company Instagram post or a team Slack channel.

Client Gifting

Individually boxed hand-piped cookies with a company logo make a premium alternative to generic branded swag. When Cooley LLP or Stripe sends a thank-you to a client, a beautifully packaged cookie box says more than a gift card. It's edible, personal, and on-brand.

Conference and Event Swag

High-volume printed cookie orders work exceptionally well as conference giveaways and trade show booth treats. Individually wrapped cookies are easy to distribute, have a shelf life that survives the event, and consistently draw people to the booth. Orders of 200, 500, and 1,000+ cookies are a regular part of the production schedule.

Employee Appreciation and Milestone Events

Retirement parties, work anniversaries, team wins, and onboarding welcome kits all benefit from a personalized touch. Logo cookies — or cookies customized with the employee's name, tenure, or team — make the moment feel considered rather than generic.

May 4th (Star Wars Day)

This is a known annual spike in the SF tech office calendar. Companies across the Bay Area order Star Wars-themed cookies and cakes for May 4th office celebrations. If your company participates, early inquiry is strongly recommended — this date books up.

None of these are off-the-shelf products. Every order is custom-designed from the client's actual brand assets — logo files, hex codes, brand guidelines. The cookies that Google's Mountain View campus receives are built from Google's actual brand, not a generic approximation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order for corporate logo cookies in San Francisco?

The minimum order is 1 dozen (12 cookies), with a minimum order value of $150. Most corporate clients order between 50 and 500 cookies per event. My Baking Creations handles orders of any size — from a boardroom meeting to a company-wide quarterly celebration.

How far in advance should I place a corporate cookie order?

Standard orders benefit from 1–2 weeks of lead time. Large orders (500+ cookies) benefit from 3–4 weeks. For time-sensitive events, contact the bakery directly at (415) 568-8060 to discuss availability.

What's the difference between hand-piped and printed logo cookies?

Hand-piped cookies are decorated entirely by hand using royal icing — artisan quality, slightly textured, ideal for bold logos and premium gifting. Printed cookies use food-safe edible ink for photographic-quality logo reproduction — ideal for complex artwork, gradients, and high-volume orders. Both methods are available; the right choice depends on your logo and use case.
