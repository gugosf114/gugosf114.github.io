About
My Baking Creations

San Francisco bakery known locally for ultra-realistic, highly artistic cake designs—serving personal celebrations and Fortune 500 companies since 2012

Scroll

Yana

Founder & Master Cake Designer

Founded in 2012 by Yana, a professional baker and cake designer from St. Petersburg, Russia, My Baking Creations has become one of the Bay Area's most trusted bakeries for ultra-realistic, highly artistic custom cake designs. Known locally for our edible art—every creation that leaves our kitchen is a handcrafted masterpiece.

Every creation that leaves our bakery — whether it's a sculpted birthday cake, branded corporate cookies, or themed cake pops — is made from scratch by hand using only the freshest, finest ingredients.

Today, My Baking Creations serves both intimate family celebrations and major corporate events across the Bay Area. From milestone birthday cakes and elegant wedding cakes to corporate logo cookies for employee appreciation and client gifting—every creation is still handcrafted by Yana and her small team using only the freshest ingredients. Our client list includes Google, Meta, Salesforce, DocuSign, Microsoft, PayPal, Alaska Airlines, Golden State Warriors, Twitch, Discord, Gap, Levi's, Cooley, and Athleta, alongside hundreds of Bay Area families celebrating life's most important moments.

Our Process

From your idea to your table — here's how it works

Step 1 of 6
1

Tell Us Your Vision

Reach out by phone, email, or our website. Share your event date, guest count, theme ideas, and any inspiration photos. Want to get started right away? Try our Sketch Your Cake AI to visualize your dream cake, or see how your logo looks on a cookie — before you even contact us.

🎂 Get a Quote Birthdays, weddings & celebrations 🏢 Corporate Quote Company events & branded treats 💬 Quick Question? Chat with us or send a message
2

Custom Sketches & Pricing

Yana creates multiple design options with pricing — typically within 24–48 hours. No commitment required. This is how every project starts.

3

Revisions & Approval

Review the sketches, pick your favorites, request changes. We go back and forth until the design is exactly what you want.

4

Payment & Scheduling

Once you approve, we send a simple invoice. Pay by credit/debit card, PayPal, or Venmo. Your spot on our calendar is locked in.

5

Handcrafted Creation

Every item is made from scratch by hand using the freshest ingredients. No shortcuts, no templates — just Yana's artistry and attention to detail.

6

Pickup or Delivery

Pick up at our Daly City kitchen (9 AM–6 PM), or we deliver anywhere in the San Francisco Bay Area.

We take pride in accommodating budgetary concerns — flexible solutions for every budget. Learn more about how to order a custom cake.
