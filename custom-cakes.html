How the Custom Cake Process Works

Getting a custom cake from My Baking Creations is a straightforward process:

1. Submit a request. Use the order form at mybakingcreations.com/order-form. Share your event date, approximate guest count, theme ideas, and any reference images you have.

2. Yana follows up. She reviews every request personally and reaches out to discuss design details, size, flavors, and confirm availability. A custom sketch is typically provided within 24–48 hours — no commitment required at that stage.

3. Design approval and production. Once the design is finalized and a deposit is placed, production begins. Every cake is made fresh to order.

Lead time matters. Standard custom cakes require at least 1–2 weeks of advance notice. Sculpted 3D cakes need 2–4 weeks, and complex multi-element builds may require 4–6 weeks. During peak seasons — graduation (May–June), the holidays (November–December), and wedding season (May–October) — booking 3–4 weeks ahead is strongly recommended.

The consultation process exists specifically to ensure the finished cake matches what the client envisioned. Yana goes back and forth on sketches until the design is right before a single ingredient is measured.

Standard custom cakes require 1–2 weeks of advance notice. Sculpted 3D cakes need 2–4 weeks. Complex multi-element sculptures may require 4–6 weeks. During graduation season (May–June), wedding season (May–October), and the holidays (November–December), booking 3–4 weeks ahead is recommended.
