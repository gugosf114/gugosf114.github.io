Frequently Asked Questions About Our Bay Area Cakes

Do you offer cake delivery near me?

Yes! We deliver throughout the San Francisco Bay Area. Our main delivery zones include San Francisco, Silicon Valley (Palo Alto, Mountain View, Menlo Park), the Peninsula, and parts of Marin County. Please fill out our order form with your address for a specific delivery quote.

How much do your custom cakes cost?

Our custom cakes are priced based on size (number of servings) and the complexity of the design. Because each cake is a unique work of art, pricing varies. Our minimum order for custom cakes starts at $250. Please request a quote for an exact price.

How far in advance should I place my order?

We recommend booking as early as possible, especially for intricate sculpted cakes or large corporate orders. A minimum of 2-3 weeks' notice is ideal, but we can sometimes accommodate rush orders depending on our schedule. For wedding cakes, we suggest 3-6 months' notice.

What flavors do you offer?

We offer a delicious menu of cake flavors and fillings, from classic vanilla bean and rich chocolate fudge to more unique options like lemon raspberry and salted caramel. You can view our full flavor list on our Flavors page.

Can you replicate a design from a photo?

Absolutely. Inspiration photos are a fantastic starting point for the design process. While we will not create an exact copy of another artist's work, we will use your photos to understand your style and create a unique My Baking Creations masterpiece just for you.
