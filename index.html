Frequently Asked Questions About Our Cakes

What types of cakes do you offer?

We specialize in a wide variety of custom cakes, including birthday cakes, corporate logo cakes, 3D sculpted and realistic cakes, tiered wedding cakes, and themed character cakes. We also offer cupcakes, cake pops, and custom cookies. Every cake is custom-designed to fit your event's theme and style.

How far in advance should I order a custom cake?

We recommend placing your order as early as possible to ensure availability. For complex sculpted or tiered cakes, we suggest 3-4 weeks' notice. For simpler custom designs, 1-2 weeks' notice is often sufficient. Please contact us for availability for last-minute requests.

Do you deliver cakes in the Bay Area?

Yes, we offer professional cake delivery throughout San Francisco, Silicon Valley, and the greater Bay Area. Our delivery service ensures your cake arrives safely and in perfect condition. Delivery fees are based on distance from our Daly City bakery.

Can you make a cake based on a picture I provide?

Absolutely! Inspiration photos are one of the best ways to communicate your vision. You can upload images directly through our order request form. While we won't replicate another artist's work exactly, we will use your images as inspiration to create a unique cake just for you.

What flavors and fillings are available for your cakes?

We offer a delicious menu of cake flavors and fillings, from classic vanilla bean and rich chocolate fudge to more unique options like red velvet, lemon raspberry, and salted caramel. All of our cakes and buttercreams are made from scratch with high-quality ingredients. You can view our full flavor list on our flavors page.
