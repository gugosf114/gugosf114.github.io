Frequently Asked Questions About Custom Birthday Cakes

How much do custom birthday cakes cost in San Francisco?

Our custom cakes are priced individually based on size (number of servings) and complexity of the design. Our sculpted and 3D cakes typically start around $250. Please fill out our quote form with your design ideas for an accurate price.

How far in advance should I order my birthday cake?

We recommend placing your order at least 2-3 weeks in advance, especially for complex 3D designs or during peak seasons. We can sometimes accommodate rush orders, so please don't hesitate to reach out to check our availability.

Do you deliver birthday cakes in San Francisco?

Yes, we offer delivery services throughout San Francisco and the entire Bay Area. Delivery fees are based on distance. You can also arrange to pick up your order from our bakery in Daly City.

What flavors and fillings do you offer?

We offer a wide variety of delicious cake flavors and fillings, from classic vanilla bean and rich chocolate fudge to more unique options like red velvet with cream cheese frosting or lemon raspberry. We can provide a full flavor menu during our design consultation.

Can you make a cake based on a photo I have?

Absolutely! We love when clients bring us inspiration photos. It's the best way for us to understand your vision. We can replicate a design or use it as a starting point to create something new and unique for you.
