Custom Logo Cookies

How we print your brand on edible art

← Back
Scroll

Custom logo cookies have become the go-to corporate gift in Silicon Valley. From Google's annual holiday parties to Meta's product launch celebrations, branded cookies are how the Bay Area's biggest companies make events memorable. But not all logo cookies are created equal. There are two fundamentally different methods for getting your brand onto a cookie, and choosing the right one makes all the difference.

Two Methods: Hand-Piped vs. Printed Logo Cookies

When companies order branded cookies for corporate events, they have two options. Each produces a beautiful result, but the technique, look, and pricing are distinct. Understanding the difference helps you choose the right method for your logo and your event.

Hand-Piped Royal Icing Cookies

Hand-piped logo cookies are decorated entirely by hand. Our artist uses royal icing to pipe and flood each cookie, painting your logo freehand with colored icing. The result has an artisanal, handcrafted quality that feels personal and elevated. Browse our hand-piped cookie gallery to see the range of designs we create.

  • Best for: Simple logos with clean lines, text-based logos, icons, and mascots
  • The look: Organic, slightly textured, with a glossy icing finish that catches light
  • Color: We mix custom icing colors to match your brand palette, though exact Pantone matching has natural limits with icing
  • Pricing: $6-$8 per cookie, reflecting the skilled hand labor involved
  • Turnaround: Each cookie takes time to pipe, flood, and dry, so plan for 2-3 weeks on larger orders

Hand-piped cookies are ideal when you want your branded treats to feel like gifts rather than marketing materials. They carry a warmth and craftsmanship that recipients notice immediately.

Printed Edible Image Cookies

Printed logo cookies use food-safe edible ink printed onto edible paper, which is then applied to a smooth royal icing base on a shortbread cookie. The result is a crisp, high-resolution reproduction of your logo with photographic accuracy. See real examples in our printed cookie gallery.

  • Best for: Complex logos, photographs, gradients, detailed illustrations, multi-color designs
  • The look: Smooth, clean, photo-quality reproduction with sharp edges and precise detail
  • Color: Exact brand color matching, including gradients, shadows, and photographic elements
  • Pricing: $5-$7 per cookie, slightly lower because the printing process is more efficient at scale
  • Turnaround: Faster production on large runs, typically 1-2 weeks for standard orders

Printed cookies are the choice when your logo has fine detail, specific color requirements, or when you need a large quantity at a competitive price point. Most of our corporate cookie orders use printed edible images.

What Makes a Good Logo Cookie

Not every logo translates perfectly to a 3-inch cookie. Here is what works best for each method:

For hand-piping: High contrast between the logo and background. Simple shapes with clean outlines. Limit yourself to 3-4 colors for the sharpest result. Text-heavy logos work well if the font is bold enough. Thin serif fonts and fine details under 2mm tend to lose definition in icing.

For printing: Almost anything works. Photographs, gradients, fine text, complex illustrations, and full-color artwork all reproduce cleanly. The main requirement is a high-resolution source file. If it looks good on screen at 300 DPI, it will look good on a cookie.

When clients are unsure which method suits their logo, we create a digital proof showing both options side by side. In many cases, we recommend printed cookies for the primary logo and hand-piped cookies for complementary designs like event names, dates, or simple thank-you messages.

Our Process: From Logo File to Cookie Box

Every custom logo cookie order follows the same proven workflow, whether you are ordering one dozen or five hundred:

  1. Submit your logo: Send us your logo file in PNG, SVG, AI, or EPS format. Include your brand color codes (Pantone or hex) and any specific requirements. Use our corporate order form or email us directly.
  2. Receive your proof: Within 1-2 business days, we send a digital mockup showing exactly how your logo will appear on the cookie. We include both method options if you are unsure which to choose.
  3. Approve and confirm: Once you approve the proof, we confirm your order quantity, delivery date, and packaging preferences. A 50% deposit secures your spot in our production schedule.
  4. Baking and decorating: We bake fresh shortbread cookies, apply the royal icing base, and either hand-pipe or print your logo. Every cookie is inspected for quality before packaging.
  5. Packaging and delivery: Cookies are individually wrapped or arranged in presentation boxes based on your preference. We deliver throughout the Bay Area or prepare your order for pickup at our Daly City kitchen.

Minimum Orders and Packaging

Our minimum order for custom logo cookies is 1 dozen (12 cookies). There is no maximum. We regularly fulfill orders of 200, 500, and even 1,000+ cookies for corporate events, conferences, and holiday gifting campaigns.

Packaging options include:

  • Individual cellophane wrapping: Each cookie sealed in a clear bag with a ribbon or heat seal. Perfect for gift bags, trade show giveaways, and office distribution.
  • Presentation boxes: Cookies arranged in branded boxes with tissue paper. Available in sets of 4, 6, or 12. Ideal for client gifts and executive presentations.
  • Bulk platter arrangement: Cookies displayed on a platter for buffet-style events. We provide the platter and arrangement at no extra charge on orders of 3 dozen or more.
  • Custom branded packaging: For recurring corporate clients, we offer boxes with your company logo printed on them. Minimum 50 boxes per run.

You can also order printed cookies online directly through our website for a streamlined experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between hand-piped and printed logo cookies?

Hand-piped logo cookies are decorated by an artist using royal icing, giving them an organic, artisanal feel. They're best for simple logos and cost $6-$8 per cookie. Printed logo cookies use edible ink on shortbread for photo-quality reproduction of complex logos with exact brand colors, costing $5-$7 per cookie.

What file format do you need for logo cookies?

We accept PNG, SVG, AI, EPS, and high-resolution JPEG files. For printed cookies, vector formats (SVG, AI, EPS) produce the sharpest results. For hand-piped cookies, any clear image of your logo works. We also need your brand color codes (Pantone or hex) if exact color matching is important.

What is the minimum order for custom logo cookies?

Our minimum order is 1 dozen (12 cookies) for both hand-piped and printed logo cookies. For corporate events and large orders, we regularly fulfill orders of 200-500+ cookies with 2-3 weeks lead time. Rush orders may be available for an additional fee.

How long do custom logo cookies stay fresh?

Our custom logo cookies stay fresh for 2-3 weeks when stored in an airtight container at room temperature. Individually wrapped cookies maintain freshness even longer, making them ideal for corporate gift bags, trade show giveaways, or shipping to remote employees.
