Cake Pops or Cake Balls — What to Order

If you have been searching for "corporate cake balls" and "corporate cake pops" interchangeably, you are not alone. They are the same core treat — bite-sized spheres of moist cake and frosting, hand-rolled and coated in a chocolate or candy shell. The difference is purely structural: cake balls are the stickless version, presented in a box, on a tray, or as part of a gift package. Cake pops have a lollipop stick, which makes them easy to hold at standing receptions and trade show booths. We produce both formats.

Why the bite-sized format works in corporate settings:

  • No utensils or plates required — guests grab one while networking or at their desk
  • Individually wrapped options make desk drops and onboarding kits effortless
  • Consistent portion size, so there is no awkward cake-cutting at a team meeting
  • Easy to distribute at scale — from a 10-person sprint celebration to a 500-person all-hands

Branding options:

  • Candy coating color-matched to your brand palette — send your hex or Pantone codes and we match the coating
  • Logo-printed toppers — edible image discs on each piece, same precision as our printed cookie line
  • Custom packaging — individual cellophane wrapping, branded boxes, or gift sets carrying your company name
  • Flavor selection — the interior cake and frosting combination is chosen at the time of your order

Every batch is made to order — no pre-made inventory. Hand-rolled and finished at our Daly City kitchen, 1096 Wildwood Ave (94015), and delivered across San Francisco (94103, 94105), South San Francisco (94080), and Mountain View (94043). Corporate Cake Pop FAQ

Cake pops or cake balls: what is the difference?

Same treat, one difference. Both are cake and frosting rolled into a bite and coated in chocolate or candy shell. Cake pops sit on a stick, cake balls do not. Both take your brand colors and a logo topper, and we make both.

How much do corporate cake pops cost?

$5 to $10 per piece, depending on coating, logo work, and packaging. The minimum order is $150. Send your count and your logo and a written quote comes back within 24 hours.

Can you put our company logo on them?

Yes. The candy coating is matched to your hex or Pantone colors, and an edible printed topper carries your logo on every piece. Send the logo as a PNG, SVG, or AI file.

How far ahead should we order?

One to two weeks for most orders. Allow two to three weeks for 100-plus pieces or custom packaging. For a tight date, call (415) 568-8060 and ask about a rush.

How are they packaged?

Individually wrapped by default, so they travel to desks and booths without a mess. Branded boxes and gift sets are available for client gifts and welcome packages.

Do you deliver to offices and trade show venues?

Yes. We deliver across San Francisco, including SoMa and the Financial District, plus South San Francisco, San Bruno, Burlingame, and Peninsula campuses in Menlo Park, Palo Alto, and Mountain View. Pickup from our Daly City kitchen is available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM.

Can different teams at our company order separately?

Yes. Departments at the same company order independently all the time, each with its own event, design, and delivery details.
