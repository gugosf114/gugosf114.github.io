Questions people ask

Can you print our company logo on cookies?

Yes. Upload your logo as a PNG, JPG or WebP, pick your brand color, and see the exact print before you pay. Corporate logo cookies come wrapped one by one, so they are easy to hand out at company events. You can also check out our corporate order request page.

Can you put a photo on a cookie?

Yes. We print your photo in edible ink on a fresh-baked cookie with white royal icing. Keep the whole photo, or remove the background so only your person or pet shows.

How much do custom cookies cost?

$5 per cookie with a 12-cookie minimum, so a dozen is $60. The gift box and individual wrapping are included. Shipping is the FedEx price for your ZIP code.

How fast do custom cookies ship?

We bake and ship within 48 hours, or you can choose your own ship date up to 90 days ahead. Pick FedEx Ground, 2Day or Standard Overnight at checkout and see the arrival date before you pay. Pickup in Daly City is free.

Do you ship outside the San Francisco Bay Area?

Yes. We ship across the United States by FedEx. For Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico we send you a shipping quote first.

What size and flavor are the cookies?

Each cookie is 3 inches, square or round, and vanilla flavored. The print is edible and safe to eat.
