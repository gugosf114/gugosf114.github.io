## Hand-Piped Royal Icing Logo Cookies — Artisan Precision for Your Brand

Hand-piped logo cookies are exactly what the name describes: every single cookie is decorated by hand, stroke by stroke, using royal icing. There is no template, no machine, and no mass-production line. A trained decorator pipes your logo freehand in colored icing, floods each section, and builds up the design layer by layer until the result matches your brand.

**What makes hand-piped cookies the premium choice:**

- **Individual attention on every cookie** — no two are mechanically identical, which is precisely the point. Recipients notice the craftsmanship immediately.
- **Brand color matching** — icing is mixed to match your palette, including Pantone and hex references, so your logo reads correctly on the cookie.
- **Custom typography and iconography** — wordmarks, icons, mascots, and custom lettering are all reproducible in royal icing by a skilled hand.
- **100% edible, made to order** — no shortcuts, no pre-made bases sitting in inventory. Every batch starts fresh when your order is confirmed.
- **No generic production** — this is not a printed sticker applied to a cookie. The design is built directly in icing on the cookie surface.

## Printed Image Logo Cookies — High-Resolution Edible Ink for Brand Consistency

For corporate buyers who need pixel-perfect logo reproduction at scale, printed image cookies deliver crisp, full-color results using food-safe edible ink printed onto edible paper, which is then applied to a smooth royal icing base on a shortbread cookie.

**What printed logo cookies do best:**

- **Complex logos with gradients, fine detail, or photography** — anything that would be difficult to reproduce by hand at small scale prints cleanly
- **Exact color accuracy** — brand colors, including multi-color gradients and photographic elements, reproduce with precision
- **Consistent output across large batches** — every cookie in a run of 200 or 500 looks identical, which matters for conference swag and onboarding kits
- **Faster turnaround on large quantities** — the printing process scales efficiently for high-volume corporate orders
