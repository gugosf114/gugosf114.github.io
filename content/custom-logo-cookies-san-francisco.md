## Frequently Asked Questions

**What's the difference between hand-piped and printed logo cookies?**
Hand-piped cookies are decorated entirely by hand using royal icing — your logo is recreated stroke by stroke by Yana. Printed cookies use food-safe edible ink printed onto edible paper, applied to a royal icing base. Hand-piped has a tactile, artisan quality; printed is better suited for complex gradients or photographic detail at high volume. Both are available — [see printed cookies here](/gallery-corporate-cookies-printed).

**What file format do I need to submit for my logo?**
PNG with a transparent background works best. SVG, AI, and EPS files are also accepted. Include your brand color codes (Pantone or hex) so the icing can be matched as closely as possible.

**Do you deliver to offices in San Francisco and Silicon Valley?**
Yes. My Baking Creations delivers directly to offices throughout San Francisco (SoMa, Financial District, Mission Bay), South San Francisco, Menlo Park, Palo Alto, Mountain View, and the broader Peninsula corridor. For high-rise buildings, delivery is coordinated with your front desk or reception.

**Do you accept purchase orders or invoices?**
Yes. Invoice and PO are accepted for corporate accounts. My Baking Creations also accepts credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Zelle. W-9s and itemized invoices are available for procurement teams.

**Can multiple departments at the same company place separate orders?**
Absolutely — and it happens regularly. Different teams at the same company often order independently for different events throughout the year. Each order is treated as its own project with its own design confirmation.
