## Branded Cakes and Cupcakes Your Team Will Actually Remember

Corporate desserts work best when they reinforce your brand and give people something to talk about. My Baking Creations produces two categories of corporate treats, each suited to different occasions and budgets:

**Hand-Piped Royal Icing Cupcakes and Cookies**
Each cupcake or cookie is individually decorated by hand using royal icing mixed to match your brand colors. The result has an artisanal, elevated quality that recipients notice — and that photographs well for internal event recaps or social media. Pricing runs $6–$8 per cookie and $5–$8 per cupcake.

**Printed Edible Image Cupcakes and Cookies**
Your logo is reproduced in high resolution using food-safe edible ink and applied to a smooth royal icing base. Ideal for complex logos, gradients, or large-quantity orders where consistency across hundreds of pieces matters. Printed cookies run $5–$7 each; cupcakes $5–$8.

**Corporate Event Cakes**
For milestone events — product launches, company anniversaries, IPO celebrations, executive retirements — a custom event cake becomes the centerpiece. These range from logo sheet cakes and tiered celebration cakes ($300–$650+) to sculpted designs like the Levi's jeans pocket cake created for an employee event. When the cake itself becomes a talking point, that's the sculpted tier.

All items are individually wrapped for easy distribution at events and can be packaged in presentation boxes for client gifting.

## How the Corporate Order Process Works

The process is designed to be low-friction for procurement teams, executive assistants, and office managers:

1. **Submit your request** — Use the [corporate order form](https://mybakingcreations.com/corporate-order.html) or email info@mybakingcreations.com with your event date, quantity, and a brief description of what you need.
2. **Share your logo and artwork** — Send your logo file (PNG, SVG, AI, or EPS) along with brand color codes. My Baking Creations handles the design translation; you don't need to provide a finished cookie mockup.
3. **Confirm design and quantity** — A digital proof is provided within 1–2 business days. Once approved, production is scheduled.
4. **Delivery or pickup** — Orders are delivered directly to your Bay Area office or picked up at the Daly City kitchen (1096 Wildwood Ave, 94015). Delivery covers all major corporate campuses across San Francisco, the Peninsula, and Silicon Valley.

**Lead time:** Standard orders require at least 1–2 weeks. Large orders (500+ pieces) benefit from 3–4 weeks. Rush orders are available for established clients — call (415) 568-8060 to discuss availability.

**Minimum order:** $150. This is a quality threshold, not a barrier — it ensures every order receives the same level of custom design attention.

**Payment:** Invoice and purchase orders (PO) accepted for corporate accounts. Credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Zelle also accepted.

**Multiple departments, no coordination required:** Different teams within the same company can order independently. There is no need to consolidate a single company-wide order — each department submits its own request.
