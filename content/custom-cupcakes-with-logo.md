## How Your Logo Gets onto Every Cupcake

There are two techniques for applying your company logo to a cupcake, and the right choice depends on your logo's design characteristics.

### Hand-Piped Royal Icing

A trained decorator pipes your logo directly onto the cupcake using custom-mixed royal icing. The result is dimensional, slightly textured, and has a premium handcrafted quality that recipients notice immediately.

- **Best for:** Bold logos with clean lines, strong geometric shapes, monograms, and wordmarks
- **Color matching:** Icing is custom-mixed by color — ideal for Pantone-matched brand palettes
- **Price point:** $6–$8 per cupcake, reflecting the skilled hand labor involved
- **Logo file:** Vector files (.ai, .eps, .svg, .pdf) produce the sharpest results; high-resolution PNG with transparent background also works well

### Edible Ink Printed Image Discs

Your logo is printed in full color onto an edible disc using food-safe edible ink, then applied to a smooth frosting base. The result is photo-realistic with precise color detail.

- **Best for:** Complex logos, gradients, multi-color artwork, photography, or detailed illustrations
- **Color matching:** Edible ink printing renders colors faithfully for most brand palettes
- **Price point:** Slightly lower than hand-piped, reflecting more efficient production at scale
- **Logo file:** Same file requirements apply — the team reviews every logo before confirming the order

**No design expertise required on your end.** The team handles file review and prep. Submit your logo file, and they will confirm whether it is production-ready or flag any adjustments needed before the order is locked in.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the minimum order for custom logo cupcakes?**
The minimum order is 1 dozen (12 cupcakes), with a $150 order minimum. Most corporate clients order 24–100+ cupcakes depending on team size and event format.

**How far in advance should I order?**
For standard corporate orders, 1–2 weeks is recommended. For large orders (100+ cupcakes) or events with specific delivery windows, 2–3 weeks gives the team the most flexibility. Rush orders are available — call (415) 568-8060 to check availability.

**Do you deliver to our office building?**
Yes. The bakery delivers directly to offices throughout San Francisco (SoMa, Financial District), South San Francisco, Mountain View, Menlo Park, Palo Alto, San Bruno, and the broader Peninsula. For high-rise buildings, the team coordinates with your front desk or reception for smooth drop-off.

**Can multiple departments at the same company place separate orders?**
Absolutely. Individual departments order independently all the time — different events, different dates, different designs. Each order is handled on its own timeline.

**Do you accept purchase orders or invoices?**
Yes. Invoice and PO are accepted for corporate accounts. The bakery also provides W-9s and itemized invoices for procurement teams. Net 30 terms are available for established accounts.

**What logo file format works best?**
Vector files (.ai, .eps, .svg, .pdf) produce the sharpest results for both hand-piped and printed techniques. High-resolution PNG with a transparent background is also a reliable option. The team reviews every file before confirming the order.

**Can you match our exact brand colors?**
For hand-piped royal icing, colors are custom-mixed and can be matched to Pantone or hex specifications. For printed edible image discs, the printing process renders most brand color palettes faithfully. Color confirmation is part of the design approval step.
