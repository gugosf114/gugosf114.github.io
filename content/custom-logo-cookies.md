## Two Ways to Put Your Brand on a Cookie: Hand-Piped vs. Edible Print

Not all logo cookies are made the same way. My Baking Creations offers two distinct methods, each with a different look, feel, and best use case. Understanding the difference helps you choose the right option for your event.

### Hand-Piped Royal Icing Cookies

A trained artist pipes your logo directly onto each cookie, color by color, using royal icing. The result is a tactile, glossy finish with visible brushwork — the kind of detail that signals a premium gift rather than a bulk giveaway.

- **Best for:** Executive client gifts, employee onboarding kits, tech company swag where perceived quality matters
- **The look:** Organic, slightly dimensional, with a handcrafted quality no machine can replicate
- **Color matching:** Custom icing colors mixed to approximate your brand palette (Pantone or HEX codes help)
- **Pricing:** $6–$8 per cookie

### Edible Ink Printed Cookies

A high-resolution digital file is printed onto a food-safe edible sheet, then applied to a hand-iced cookie base. The result is photo-accurate logo reproduction — sharp edges, consistent color, and the ability to handle gradients, fine text, and complex artwork.

- **Best for:** Photo-accurate logos, large quantities, tighter production deadlines
- **The look:** Clean, flat, photographic — identical from cookie to cookie
- **Color matching:** Exact brand color reproduction, including gradients and multi-color artwork
- **Pricing:** $5–$6 per cookie

## What to Send Us: File Formats, Logo Guidelines & Artwork Tips

Getting your logo onto a cookie starts with the right file. Here is exactly what to prepare before you place your order.

**Accepted file formats:**
- **Preferred:** PNG with transparent background
- **Also accepted:** AI, EPS, PDF, SVG
- **For printed method only:** JPG is accepted

**Resolution guidance for edible printing:**
- 300 DPI or higher at the intended print size produces the sharpest result
- If your file looks crisp at full size on screen, it will print cleanly on a cookie

**For hand-piped orders:**
- A clean vector or high-contrast logo works best
- Intricate gradients are interpreted by the artist — the result is a skilled translation, not a pixel-perfect reproduction
- Simple logos with clean lines and 3–4 colors yield the sharpest hand-piped results

**Brand color matching:**
- Provide Pantone or HEX codes when color accuracy is critical — this is standard practice for tech company brand guidelines
- Clients including Google, Meta, Stripe, and Salesforce send their brand files directly; their brand teams receive finished cookies that meet their standards

**What happens after you submit:**
The order form collects your logo file, quantity, event date, and delivery zip code. No back-and-forth guessing — everything is captured upfront so your quote is accurate from the start.
