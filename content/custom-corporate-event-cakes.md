## How the Custom Corporate Cake Process Works

The process is designed to be straightforward for busy event coordinators and executive assistants.

**Step 1 — Submit your order request**
Use the [order request form](https://mybakingcreations.com/order-form) to share your event date, expected headcount, logo or branding assets, and any theme details. This is not a commitment — it's the starting point.

**Step 2 — Design consultation and confirmation**
We review your brief and follow up to confirm the design direction, cake size, and timeline. The earlier you reach out, the more flexibility we have on design complexity and customization.

**Step 3 — Hand-crafted production**
Your cake is made to spec by hand. No shortcuts, no pre-made components. Every element — from the logo recreation to the color matching — is executed during production.

**Step 4 — Pickup or delivery**
Your order is ready for pickup at our Daly City kitchen (1096 Wildwood Ave, 94015) or delivered directly to your office or event venue.

We're based in Daly City (94015) and regularly deliver to San Bruno (94066), Burlingame (94010), and San Mateo (94401), in addition to San Francisco and Silicon Valley. If you're coordinating an event with a tight timeline, early inquiry gives us the best opportunity to accommodate your needs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What types of corporate events do you make cakes for?**
We make cakes for product launches, company anniversaries, retirement parties, employee appreciation events, office holiday parties, client gifting events, team milestone celebrations, and onboarding welcome events. If your company is marking an occasion, we can design a cake around it.

**Can you recreate our company logo on a cake?**
Yes. We hand-craft logo elements directly onto cakes using royal icing, fondant, and modeling chocolate. For printed applications, we also offer high-resolution edible image printing. Both methods are available depending on the design and scale of your order.

**Do you deliver to corporate campuses and office buildings?**
Yes. We deliver to offices and corporate campuses throughout San Francisco, the Peninsula, and Silicon Valley — including high-rise buildings in the Financial District, tech campuses in Mountain View and Menlo Park, and the South San Francisco biotech corridor. We coordinate with your reception or facilities team for smooth delivery.

**How far in advance should we place a corporate cake order?**
The earlier you inquire, the more design flexibility we can offer. We recommend reaching out as soon as your event date is confirmed. Rush orders may be available depending on current production schedule — contact us directly at (415) 568-8060 to discuss.

**Do you accept purchase orders or invoices for corporate accounts?**
Yes. We accept purchase orders, invoices, credit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Zelle. We can provide W-9s and itemized invoices for procurement teams.

**What is the minimum order?**
Our minimum order is $150. There are no budget tiers — every order receives the same standard of craftsmanship.

**Can different departments at the same company place separate orders?**
Absolutely. Multiple departments within the same organization order from us independently throughout the year. We're set up to handle recurring orders from the same company across different teams, events, and timelines.
