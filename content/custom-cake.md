## Custom Cake Styles We Create: From Birthdays to Corporate Events

The bakery handles a wide range of cake types, each requiring a different set of skills and a different approach to design.

### Hyper-Realistic Sculpted Cakes for Luxury Occasions

Sculpted cakes are the pinnacle of custom cake artistry — fully three-dimensional, structurally engineered, and designed to look like something other than a cake until the moment it's cut.

Past examples include:
- A **Ferrari cake** for a car collector, with hand-sculpted bodywork, painted detail, and edible wheels
- A **Nike SB Dunk shoe cake**, with realistic laces, sole texture, and colorway accuracy
- A **full sushi platter cake**, with individual nigiri, rolls, and garnishes — every element edible

These cakes are not for the price-sensitive buyer. They represent the highest level of custom cake work available in the Bay Area, and the clients who order them — often from affluent communities in **Tiburon, Ross, San Rafael, and Petaluma** in Marin County — are looking for something that stops a room.

If you're considering a sculpted cake, [browse the gallery](https://mybakingcreations.com/gallery-cakes-sculpted) to see the range of what's possible before reaching out.

### Corporate Event Cakes for Milestones and Celebrations

Custom cakes for company events are a distinct category with their own design language. These range from sheet cakes and decorated tiers featuring company logos and brand colors to more elaborate sculpted pieces — like the **Levi's jeans pocket cake** created for an employee event.

Common use cases include:
- Company anniversaries and milestone celebrations
- Product launch parties and go-live events
- Employee retirement send-offs with career-themed designs
- Office celebrations tied to funding rounds, acquisitions, or major deals
- **Star Wars Day (May 4th)** themed orders — a significant spike in corporate cake demand from Bay Area tech companies

Departments within the same company often order independently, making corporate cake clients some of the most valuable repeat accounts in the business.

### Character and Theme Birthday Cakes

For personal birthday celebrations, character cakes are among the most requested designs. Current high-demand themes include:

- **Star Wars, Marvel, Pokemon** — consistently popular across age groups
- **Disney Princess, Frozen, Barbie** — perennial favorites for younger celebrations

Whether the birthday is for a five-year-old or a fifty-year-old, the design process is the same: a custom sketch, your approval, and a finished cake built specifically for that person.

## How to Order a Custom Cake from My Baking Creations

The ordering process is straightforward and designed to reduce friction at every step.

**Step 1: Submit your order request**
Fill out the [order request form](https://mybakingcreations.com/order-form) with your event date, approximate guest count, theme or design ideas, and any reference images you have. The more detail you provide, the faster the consultation moves.

**Step 2: Receive your design consultation and quote**
Yana reviews your request and responds — typically within 24 to 48 hours — with design sketches and a pricing estimate. There's no commitment required at this stage. The sketch is part of the process, not a sales pitch.

**Step 3: Approve your design and confirm your order**
Once you're happy with the design direction, you confirm the order and secure your date. From there, the team handles production. You don't need to follow up or check in — you'll hear from us when your cake is ready.

**Lead time:** Custom cakes generally require **2 to 3 weeks** of advance notice. Sculpted 3D cakes may require 2 to 4 weeks depending on complexity. Booking early is strongly recommended for weekend dates and peak seasons (graduation in May–June, holidays in November–December).
