## What Is a Hyper-Realistic Sculpted Cake?

A hyper-realistic sculpted cake is not a decorated cake with a printed image on top. It is a three-dimensional, structurally engineered object — built entirely from cake, fondant, modeling chocolate, and gum paste — that looks exactly like a real-world thing.

A Ferrari. A Nike sneaker. A full sushi platter. A designer handbag. When guests walk into the room, they see the object. They do not see a cake. That is the point.

Every single element is edible. There are no plastic props, no foam inserts, no shortcuts. The laces on a sneaker cake are made from gum paste. The tuna on a nigiri cake is sculpted fondant. The headlights on a car cake are hand-painted with edible luster dust. When the knife finally goes in, the reveal is the moment everyone in the room remembers.

## The Sculpting Process: From Concept to Edible Masterpiece

Hyper-realistic sculpted cakes are made-to-order commissions. Here is how the process works from inquiry to delivery.

**Step 1 — Consultation**
Submit your request through the [order request form](https://mybakingcreations.com/order-form). Include reference photos, the object or theme you have in mind, approximate size, event date, and any personalization details. The more specific your brief, the more accurate the initial quote.

**Step 2 — Design Review**
Yana reviews the brief, confirms feasibility, and provides a quote based on complexity, size, and the materials required. Sculpted cakes start at $300 for simpler shaped designs; complex multi-element sculptures with hand-painted details range from $400 to $650 and above. Each piece is quoted individually.

**Step 3 — Structural Build**
Internal cake layers are carved and stacked to form the base shape. Fondant, gum paste, modeling chocolate, and rice cereal treats are used to sculpt fine details and achieve forms that would be impossible with standard cake construction. The structure is built to hold — and to be fully edible when served.

**Step 4 — Painting and Finishing**
Food-safe edible paints, luster dusts, and airbrushing bring color, texture, and realism to life. This is the stage that transforms a shaped cake into something that genuinely fools the eye. Metallic finishes, matte surfaces, translucent glazes — every effect is achieved with edible materials.

**Step 5 — Delivery or Pickup**
Given the structural complexity of sculpted cakes, personal delivery is strongly recommended over pickup. My Baking Creations delivers across San Francisco, Daly City (94015), South San Francisco (94080), San Bruno (94066), Burlingame (94010), San Mateo (94401), Palo Alto (94301), and surrounding Bay Area locations. Delivery is included within 20 miles of the Daly City studio.

**Lead Time**
Sculpted cakes require a minimum of 2–4 weeks advance notice. Complex multi-element pieces may require 4–6 weeks. Contact the bakery as early as possible to secure your date.

Each piece is quoted individually after a review of your design brief. Contact the bakery at [info@mybakingcreations.com](mailto:info@mybakingcreations.com) or (415) 568-8060 for a custom quote.

**How far in advance do I need to order?**
A minimum of 2–4 weeks is required for most sculpted cakes. Complex multi-element pieces may need 4–6 weeks. The earlier you reach out, the better — weekend dates and peak seasons fill quickly.

**Is everything on the cake actually edible?**
The vast majority of every sculpted cake is fully edible — real cake layers, buttercream filling, fondant, modeling chocolate, and gum paste. For very tall or structurally demanding designs, food-safe internal supports (dowels and boards) may be used; these are removed before serving.
