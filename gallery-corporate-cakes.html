How To Order In Five Steps

  1. Send the request. Use the corporate quote form with your event date, head count, and delivery address.
  2. Share the design. Logo as PNG, SVG, or PDF, brand colors as hex or Pantone. For a sculpted cake, send reference photos from a few angles.
  3. Get your quote. A written quote usually lands within 24 hours.
  4. Approve the design. Confirm the look and the size, and your date is locked into production.
  5. Delivery or pickup. Hand-delivered presentation-ready to your office or venue, or pick up in Daly City, Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM.

Corporate Cake FAQ

How much does a corporate cake cost?

Corporate cakes run $250 to $650-plus, depending on size, design, and how much of it is sculpted or hand-painted. Sculpted replicas start at $300. Send your head count and idea and a written quote comes back within 24 hours.

Can you put our company logo on the cake?

Yes. The logo goes on as a high-resolution edible printed image, or piped and painted by hand for a raised, textured look. Send the logo as a PNG, SVG, or PDF, with hex or Pantone codes if the colors must match exactly.

Can you make a cake shaped like our product?

Yes. Phones, sneakers, software boxes, bottles, mascots and buildings have all been sculpted in cake. Send reference photos from a few angles. Allow one to two weeks; complex sculpted designs run $400 to $650-plus.

How far ahead should we order?

A week ahead is comfortable. One to two weeks is ideal for sculpted, photo, or large tiered cakes. Rush orders with same-day Bay Area delivery are sometimes possible; call (415) 568-8060 with your date.

Do you deliver to offices and venues?

Yes. Cakes are hand-delivered, presentation-ready, to offices and venues in San Francisco, Daly City, South San Francisco, Palo Alto, Mountain View, Cupertino, Sunnyvale, and San Jose. Nobody on your team has to assemble anything. Pickup from our Daly City kitchen is available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM.

Can we add matching cupcakes, cookies, or cake pops?

Yes. Full dessert spreads for 50 to 500 people are common at launches: the cake as the centerpiece, plus logo cupcakes, logo cookies, and cake pops in the same colors, all from one kitchen for one event date.

Do you take purchase orders and invoices?

Yes. Purchase orders, itemized invoices, credit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Zelle are all accepted. Different departments at the same company can each place their own orders.
