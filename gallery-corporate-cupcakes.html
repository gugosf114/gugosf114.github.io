How Branded Cupcake Orders Work

Every cupcake we produce for a corporate event can carry your company's exact logo, brand colors, or event theme. Two decoration methods are available depending on your design and quantity:

  • Hand-piped royal icing — each cupcake decorated individually by hand, matched to your brand palette. Best for bold logos, clean icons, and designs where the artisan quality is part of the impression.
  • Edible printed toppers — high-resolution edible ink reproduces your logo with photographic precision. Ideal for complex artwork, gradients, or large orders where consistency across every cupcake matters.

These are made-to-order products. There is no pre-made inventory, no frozen product, and no generic decoration pulled from a template — the same standard Google, Meta, Stripe, Salesforce, and DocuSign have trusted since 2012.

Orders are fulfilled from our kitchen at 1096 Wildwood Ave, Daly City 94015, with direct delivery to corporate campuses across SoMa (94103), the Financial District (94105), South San Francisco (94080), San Bruno, Burlingame, San Mateo, Menlo Park, Mountain View, and Palo Alto.

Typical occasions: product launches, employee appreciation days, client gifting, new-hire onboarding kits, company anniversaries, office holiday parties, and all-hands meetings. Individual departments within the same company routinely order separately — HR for onboarding one week, marketing for a launch the next.

To order, send your event date, quantity, and delivery address through the order form, along with your logo in PNG, SVG, or PDF and your brand colors in Pantone or hex. A custom quote typically comes back within 24 hours. Full details on corporate cupcake orders.

How Corporate Cupcake Orders Work at My Baking Creations

Placing a corporate cupcake or cake order is designed to be low-friction for busy teams. Here's how the process works:

  1. Submit a request. Fill out the order request form with your event date, quantity, and any initial design notes.
  2. Share your brand assets. Send over your logo files, brand color codes, or any event-specific artwork. High-resolution files produce the sharpest results for printed toppers.
  3. Review a design proof. Before production begins, you'll see what the finished product will look like and have the chance to request adjustments.
  4. Confirm and schedule. Once the design is approved, production is scheduled. You choose pickup from Daly City (94015) or arrange delivery to your location.

Orders are fulfilled for groups of any size — from a small team of 20 to a company-wide event requiring several hundred individually portioned pieces. Lead time matters: reaching out at least one to two weeks before your event date is strongly recommended, and even earlier around product launch cycles or high-demand periods like end-of-quarter celebrations. Delivery covers the full Bay Area, including 94066 San Bruno, 94010 Burlingame, 94401 San Mateo, 94122 Outer Sunset SF, and 94112 Ingleside and Excelsior SF, in addition to Silicon Valley and Peninsula campuses.

The most creative corporate clients don't limit branded cupcakes to internal celebrations.
