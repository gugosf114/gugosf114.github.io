Realistic & Sculpted Cakes That Stop the Room

This is where My Baking Creations is known throughout the Bay Area.

Hyper-realistic sculpted cakes are designed to look exactly like something other than a cake — until you cut into them. Past creations include:

  • A Ferrari sculpted for a car collector — every panel, every detail in edible fondant
  • A Nike SB Dunk sneaker — laces, sole, tongue, and all
  • A full sushi platter — individual rolls, nigiri, and chopsticks, all cake
  • Designer handbags, Faberge eggs, military tanks, chess sets

Every single element is edible. No plastic figures, no store-bought toppers, no filler. The sculpting process uses layered cake structure with fondant, modeling chocolate, and rice cereal treats to achieve shapes that look structurally impossible but taste exactly as good as they look.

Customers who order these cakes are not price-sensitive — they want something genuinely extraordinary, and they find it here. These are statement pieces for milestone birthdays, collector celebrations, and anyone who wants their cake to be the most talked-about moment of the event.

How to Order Your Custom Cake

The ordering process is straightforward and designed around your schedule.

Step 1: Submit an Order Request

Start at mybakingcreations.com/order-form.html. Share your event date, theme or design ideas, approximate party size, and whether you need pickup or delivery. Reference photos are always helpful — a Pinterest board, a character image, or a product photo all give the design process a clear starting point.

Step 2: Design Consultation

After your inquiry comes in, Yana reviews the details and follows up — typically within 24 hours. This is where the back-and-forth happens: confirming the design direction, discussing size and serving count, and aligning on any specific details that matter to you.

Step 3: Design Approval Before Baking Begins

For corporate orders and complex designs, a mockup or proof is reviewed before production starts. Nothing goes into the oven until you're satisfied with the direction.

Step 4: Pickup or Delivery

Pickup is available at the Daly City kitchen (1096 Wildwood Ave, 94015) or the Outer Sunset SF location (1800 23rd Ave, 94122). Delivery covers the full Bay Area — from Marin County across the Golden Gate to San Jose in the South Bay.

A note on minimums: My Baking Creations has a minimum order threshold. This is a premium, made-to-order bakery — not a budget option. If you're looking for something handcrafted and genuinely custom, you're in the right place.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order a custom cake?

For most birthday and celebration cakes, 1–2 weeks is the standard lead time. Sculpted 3D cakes and complex designs require 2–4 weeks. Corporate orders with large quantities benefit from 2 weeks or more. The earlier you reach out, the more design options are available.

Do you deliver to San Francisco?

Yes. My Baking Creations delivers throughout San Francisco, including SoMa (94103), the Financial District (94105), the Sunset District (94122), and the Ingleside/Excelsior corridor (94112). An Outer Sunset pickup location at 1800 23rd Ave is also available.

Can I order a cake that looks like a specific object or character?

Yes — this is one of the bakery's specialties. Past sculpted cakes have included sneakers, luxury cars, designer handbags, sushi platters, and dozens of character designs. Send a reference image through the order form and the team will confirm what's possible for your date.

Do corporate clients need to go through a single point of contact?

No. Individual departments within the same company can place independent orders without coordinating through a central contact. This is a common pattern among the bakery's tech and fintech clients.
