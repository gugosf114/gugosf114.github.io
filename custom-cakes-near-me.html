How the Custom Cake Ordering Process Works

First-time custom cake buyers often have the same question: what do I actually need to do to place an order? Here's exactly how it works at My Baking Creations:

Step 1: Submit Your Order Request

The process starts at the order request form at mybakingcreations.com/order-form. Share your event date, approximate guest count, theme ideas, and any inspiration photos you have. You don't need to have everything figured out — a general idea is enough to get started.

Step 2: Design Consultation

Yana reviews your request and typically responds within 24–48 hours with design sketches and a pricing estimate. You'll have options to choose from, and there's no commitment required at this stage. The consultation is collaborative — Yana guides you through design choices based on your vision, your budget, and what's achievable within your timeline.

Step 3: Approve and Confirm

Once you're happy with the design and pricing, you confirm the order and pay a deposit to secure your date on the production calendar. From there, Yana handles everything.

Lead time to keep in mind:

  • Standard custom cakes: 1–2 weeks minimum
  • Sculpted 3D cakes: 2–4 weeks recommended
  • Complex multi-element sculptures: 4–6 weeks

Before you even reach out, you can browse hundreds of real product photos in the gallery at mybakingcreations.com/gallery. The gallery has a search bar — type in a keyword (like "Star Wars," "sneaker," or "logo cake") to find designs similar to what you have in mind.
