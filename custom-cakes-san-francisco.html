How to Order a Custom Cake from My Baking Creations

Ordering a custom cake for the first time can feel uncertain — especially when the event matters. Here's exactly how the process works, so you know what to expect at every step.

1

Submit your order request

Fill out the order request form with your event date, the type of cake or treat you're looking for, your preferred delivery or pickup location, and any inspiration images you have. The more detail you can share upfront, the better — but even a rough idea is enough to get started. You can also reach us directly at info@mybakingcreations.com or (415) 568-8060.

2

We discuss your design and confirm the details

Yana will follow up to talk through your design vision, event logistics, timeline, and any specifics about the order. For corporate clients, this is when brand guidelines, color matching, and quantity are confirmed. For personal orders, this is where your cake concept takes shape. You'll get a clear quote before anything moves forward — no surprises.

3

Your cake is hand-crafted to order

Once the design and timeline are confirmed, production begins. Everything is made by hand, from scratch, specifically for your order. No pre-made bases, no shortcuts. Standard lead time for most cakes is 1–2 weeks. Sculpted and hyper-realistic cakes typically require 2–4 weeks. For corporate cookie orders, plan for at least 1–2 weeks. The earlier you reach out, the more flexibility we have to get the design exactly right.

4

Pickup or delivery on your event date

Choose pickup at our main kitchen in Daly City (1096 Wildwood Ave, 94015), or opt for direct delivery to your event location anywhere in the Bay Area. For corporate orders, we deliver directly to your office building or campus. Delivery fees vary by distance and order size — ask when you inquire.

We maintain a minimum order threshold that reflects the premium, made-to-order nature of every item we produce. This isn't the place for a last-minute, bare-bones cake — and if you're reading this page, that's probably not what you're looking for either. It's the place for a cake that does justice to the occasion.
