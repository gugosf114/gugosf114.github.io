Custom Corporate Cakes Built Around Your Brand

Every corporate cake from My Baking Creations is made to order from scratch. What corporate cakes look like in practice:

  • Sheet cakes for large office gatherings — decorated with company logos, team names, or celebration messaging. Sized to feed 20 to 100+ people, these are the workhorses of office milestone events: quarterly wins, work anniversaries, all-hands celebrations.
  • Sculpted and 3D cakes for high-impact moments — product launches, executive milestones, and IPO celebrations call for something more than a decorated rectangle. My Baking Creations has built sculpted cakes shaped like products, brand icons, and company mascots for clients who want the cake to be the moment.
  • Brand color matching — Pantone and hex codes are matched as closely as possible in fondant, buttercream, and royal icing. Send your brand guidelines or a screenshot of your company website and the team handles the rest.

How the Corporate Order Process Works

My Baking Creations has been serving Bay Area corporate clients since 2012. It's a family-owned bakery, not a chain — which means the same quality standard applies whether you're ordering for a 12-person team or a 500-person company event.

The order flow:

1. Submit an inquiry — use the corporate order request form or email info@mybakingcreations.com. A logo file, brand guidelines, or even a screenshot of your company website is enough to get started.

2. Receive a custom quote — typically within 24 hours. The quote covers design, quantity, packaging, and delivery.

3. Approve the design — a digital proof is provided for logo items before production begins.

4. Delivery or pickup — orders are delivered directly to your office, or available for pickup at the Daly City kitchen (9 AM–6 PM, Monday–Saturday).

On timelines:

Standard turnaround is 1–2 weeks. Rush orders are accommodated where the production schedule allows — contact the bakery early if your event date is tight. For large orders (500+ pieces), 3–4 weeks is recommended.

Geography covered:

My Baking Creations delivers throughout San Francisco, the Peninsula corridor, and Silicon Valley — including Mountain View (94043), Palo Alto (94301), and San Mateo (94401). Corporate campus deliveries to specific building numbers are handled regularly for clients at Google, Meta, and other major campuses.

Payment options accepted:

Invoice and purchase orders (PO) for established corporate accounts. Also accepted: credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Zelle. W-9s and itemized invoices are available for procurement teams.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order for corporate cakes in San Francisco?

The minimum order is $150 across all products. Most corporate cake orders fall well above this threshold — a standard decorated sheet cake for a team of 20–30 starts around $300–$650 depending on design complexity.

How far in advance should I order a corporate cake?

Standard turnaround is 1–2 weeks. For large orders or complex sculpted designs, 3–4 weeks is recommended. Rush orders are available — contact the bakery directly at (415) 568-8060 to discuss your timeline.

Do you deliver to offices in SoMa and the Financial District?

Yes. My Baking Creations delivers regularly to offices throughout SoMa (94103) and the Financial District (94105). For high-rise buildings, delivery is coordinated with your front desk or reception team.

Can different departments at the same company place separate orders?

Absolutely. Multiple teams within the same company order independently — this is one of the most common patterns among My Baking Creations' corporate clients. Each order is treated as its own project with its own design and delivery logistics.

What file formats do you need for logo reproduction?

PNG, SVG, AI, or EPS files work best. Brand color codes (Pantone or hex) help with accurate color matching. A screenshot of your company website is also a workable starting point if formal brand assets aren't available.

Do you accept purchase orders and invoices?

Yes. Invoice and PO payment is accepted for established corporate accounts. W-9s and itemized invoices are available for procurement teams. Credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Zelle are also accepted.
