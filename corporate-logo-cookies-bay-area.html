Printed Image Cookies for Corporate Events & Gifting

For large-volume orders, product launches, and onboarding swag kits, printed image cookies offer a different approach — and the same premium quality.

Edible ink is printed directly onto a smooth royal icing base on a shortbread cookie, reproducing your logo with photographic precision. Complex artwork, gradients, multi-color designs, and detailed brand marks all translate cleanly at scale. Every cookie in a batch of 500 looks identical — which matters when you're distributing to a full company all-hands or a trade show booth.

Printed cookies are the right choice when you need:

  • High-volume consistency across a large order
  • Complex logos with gradients, fine detail, or photographic elements
  • Faster turnaround on bulk quantities
  • Pixel-perfect brand color matching across every piece

The same corporate clients who order hand-piped cookies for executive gifting often use printed image cookies for larger events — two tools for two different use cases, both made to order in our Daly City kitchen.

How Corporate Ordering Works at My Baking Creations

The process is straightforward. No phone tag, no complicated back-and-forth.

Step 1: Submit your inquiry

Use the corporate order form or email info@mybakingcreations.com. Include your logo file (PNG, SVG, AI, or EPS), brand colors in Pantone or hex, your event date, and your approximate headcount.

Step 2: Receive your quote

Corporate quotes typically come back within 24 hours. For logo items, a design proof or mockup is provided for approval before production begins.

Step 3: Production and delivery

Standard lead time for corporate orders is 1–2 weeks. For large orders — 500+ pieces — two weeks gives the team time to match brand colors precisely across every cookie. Rush orders are available for established clients; call (415) 568-8060 to discuss timeline.

Payment options accepted:

Invoice and purchase orders (PO) are accepted for corporate accounts. Credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Zelle are also available.

A few things worth knowing:

  • Made to order — there is no pre-made inventory. Every batch is crafted specifically for your event.
  • Family-owned since 2012 — not a chain, not a fulfillment center. You work directly with the baker.
  • Individually wrapped — all cookies and cake pops arrive presentation-ready, with no additional setup required from your team.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I place a corporate cookie order?

My Baking Creations recommends at least one to two weeks for most corporate orders. For large orders over 500 pieces, two weeks allows time to match brand colors precisely across every cookie. Rush orders are available — call (415) 568-8060 to check availability.

Do you accept purchase orders or invoices?

Yes. Invoice and PO payment are accepted for corporate accounts. Credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Zelle are also available.

Can different departments at the same company place separate orders?

Absolutely — and they regularly do. Different teams, different floors, different events can each place independent orders without coordinating through a single point of contact.

What logo file formats do you accept?

PNG, SVG, AI, EPS, and PDF. Vector files (SVG, AI, EPS) are preferred for hand-piped orders. For printed image cookies, a high-resolution PNG with a transparent background works well.

Do you deliver to Silicon Valley and Peninsula offices?

Yes. My Baking Creations delivers to corporate campuses throughout San Francisco, South San Francisco, San Bruno, Burlingame, San Mateo, Menlo Park, Palo Alto, Mountain View, and beyond.

Can I order a mix of cookies and a cake for the same event?

Yes. Mixed orders — combining logo cookies, cake pops, cupcakes, and a custom event cake — are common for product launches and company milestone events. Mention your full needs when you submit the inquiry and a combined quote will be provided.
