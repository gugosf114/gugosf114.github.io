What Makes These Cookies Stand Out: The Hand-Piped Difference

Not all decorated cookies are made the same way. The difference between a hand-piped royal icing cookie and a mass-produced decorated cookie is visible the moment you see them side by side.

Hand-piped royal icing cookies:

  • Each cookie is flooded and layered by hand using royal icing
  • Colors are mixed to match your brand palette — clean lines, dimensional detail, glossy finish
  • The result has an artisanal quality that recipients notice immediately
  • Best suited for logos with clean shapes, bold text, icons, and mascots
  • Priced at the Tier 1 level, reflecting the skilled hand labor involved

Printed image cookies:

  • Your logo or image is printed using professional-grade edible ink onto edible paper, applied over a smooth royal icing base
  • Delivers photographic accuracy — gradients, fine detail, complex multi-color artwork all reproduce cleanly
  • Ideal for intricate logos, portraits, and designs where exact color matching is critical
  • Available at a slightly lower price point than hand-piped, making them efficient for larger runs

Both formats are available within the same order. A common approach for corporate events: hand-piped cookies featuring the company logo as the primary gift item, paired with printed cookies for event-specific messaging or team photos.

Real product photos of both styles are available in our gallery at mybakingcreations.com/gallery-cookies. Use the gallery search bar to browse by keyword — search your industry, your theme, or your occasion to find relevant examples.
